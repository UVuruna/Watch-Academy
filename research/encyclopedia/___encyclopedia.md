Reference screenshots and rendered-diagram captures used while drafting the
Encyclopedia's content (owner review material) — not part of the app
runtime, no `.md` of its own; images only.

- 🖼️ `1_home.png`, `2_themes_cube.png`, `2_themes_divine.png`,
  `3_article_greek_pantheon.png`, `3_article_greek_planetary.png`,
  `3_article_guide.png` — in-app screenshots of the Home screen, the Themes
  wholes and three article pages, captured for owner review while the
  Encyclopedia content was drafted (see
  [Encyclopedia Expansion](../encyclopedia_expansion.md))

  (`axis_lion_lamb`, `axis_sacred`, `banknote`, `cube`, `domy_safe`,
  `double_trinity`, `fall_star`, `hexagram`, `sixty_five_terms`,
  `thirteen_axes`, `three_sets`, `two_crosses`) — owner-facing captures of
  the computed diagrams described in [The Cube Canon](../../CUBE.md)
