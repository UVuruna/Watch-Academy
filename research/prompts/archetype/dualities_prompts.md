# Dualities Prompts — the Two Great Oppositions Given Their Own Images

The abstract-duality art set (owner-sealed 2026-07-22, RESTRUCTURE
§New Content). Where the [Triads](triads_prompts.md) give the trio's
stacked readings their images, this sheet gives the two great DUAL
readings theirs — **Good / Evil** and **Self / Others** (love-for-self
and love-for-others) — per the new art doctrine (see
[The DOMY Canon](../../../CANON.md), §The abstract triads & dualities
art doctrine: **every stacked reading gets its own image**). A duality
is not a trio: there is no crown that holds both, only the two poles and
the razor's edge between them.

**The two pairs:**

| Pair | Poles | The edge between |
|---|---|---|
| **Good / Evil** | Good (radiant, giving, rising) vs Evil (starless, devouring, sinking) | the choice — the same act turned to build or to ruin |
| **Self / Others** | Self (love turned inward) vs Others (love turned outward) | **the CLEAR HEART** where the two loves meet without tipping into sin |

**Self / Others rides the One_Love doctrine.** The Prism paint summit
`One_Love` (see [Persons Prompts](persons_prompts.md)) is defined as
*"the place where love of self and love of others meet without tipping
into sin,"* drawn as a heart of PERFECTLY CLEAR uncolored glass. This
pair splits that summit into its two halves and keeps the clear heart as
the razor's edge each pole leans toward: **Self** is love drawn to one's
own breast (the warm red of Pride's arm, but held BEFORE the sin — the
right self-regard the summit needs), **Others** is love reaching outward
to the world (the cool blue of Hope's arm — the gift held before it
becomes self-erasure). Neither pole is a vice; the sin is only the
TIP past the clear heart, in either direction. Both windows carry the
clear heart so the pair reads as the two arms of One_Love pulled apart.

**Register:** the house night-window stained-glass family — the SAME
register as the Prism persons and the Trinity lancets. Each pole is TWO
images: a **TALL pointed-arch lancet** (aspect 1:2) and a **1:1 circle
companion** condensing the lancet's headline scene, per the badge system
(see [Badge 1:1 Prompts](../badge/badge_1to1_prompts.md)). The circle
keeps the lancet's exact palette and border motif.

**Drop paths:** `assets/archetypes/dualities/<pair>/<Figure>.png` (the
lancets) and `assets/archetypes/dualities/<pair>/circle/<Figure>.png`
(the circle companions) — `<pair>` is `good_evil` or `self_others`;
sourceless (PromptPainter appends `_gem`/`_gpt`). Stems: `Good`, `Evil`,
`Self`, `Others`.

**House rules carried from every other sheet:** photorealistic render,
isolated background, the window shape IS the frame, NO lettering
anywhere in any image.
