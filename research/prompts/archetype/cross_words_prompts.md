# Cross Words Prompts — the Eight Words Behind the Station Letters

The CROSS-WORDS round (owner UV inbox 2026-07-27, CANON.md §The
Banknote — The Cross Rings): the DOMY and PILOT ring letters each
INITIAL their own station, and the hover legend names the classical
WORD that carries the link — Y *Ysteria*, Ω *Orgē*, D *Detestatio*,
M *Miseria* on the dark cross; Θ *Tharsos*, L *Latria*, Π *Pothos*,
Ω *Ōpheleia* on the light one. These eight words are the NEW legend
concepts of that round; this sheet gives each its own image — the
word as a figure, not the station as a scene.

**This sheet is NOT the stations.** The eight station lancets and
circles (Fear, Anger, Hate, Suffering; Hope, Faith, Love, Salvation)
live in [Two Crosses Prompts](crosses_prompts.md) and stay there. A
STATION is where the traveler stands at that hour; a WORD is the
classical name the ring letter borrows to point at it. One is a scene
on the road, the other a personification out of Greek and Latin —
different subject, different image, no file shared (the
one-image-one-place law).

**Register & look (the tree law, `assets/___assets.md`):** the
**secondary** register of the crosses family, **colored** look — the
stations hold `primary`/`circle`; the words are their second reading,
which is exactly what a register is. Every image is a **1:1 ROUND
medallion** of the house night-window family. Sheets state canonical
SOURCE-LESS paths; PromptPainter appends the `_gem`/`_gpt` suffix.

**Drop path:** `assets/archetypes/crosses/secondary/colored/`. Stems,
PascalCase, transliterated without diacritics (exactly as the ring
legend prints them): `Ysteria`, `Orge`, `Detestatio`, `Miseria`,
`Tharsos`, `Latria`, `Pothos`, `Opheleia`.

## THE HOUSE LETTERING RULE — read before generating

**NO lettering inside any image** — no Greek, no Latin, no ring
letters. The Y/Ω/D/M and Θ/L/Π/Ω letters themselves already exist as
the ring's own PNG letter library and are never painted into glass;
the words' spellings are legend and article material.

---

## Rule #19 derivation check (written, root `CLAUDE.md` Rule #19)

**IRREDUCIBLE — generated (8 files):** eight personifications that
exist nowhere else as images. None is a re-render of its station: the
station shows the WALKER at an hour of the road (a figure stopped at
dusk, a table set at evening); the word shows the CLASSICAL FIGURE the
letter names (the panic itself, the longing itself). No master
transforms into any of them.

**COMPUTED — never generated:** tints, metals, the Thematic ring
recolor (cross_red/cross_blue live in `recolor/presets/metals.json`),
lit/ghost opacity — palette and transform math on the same files. The
ring LETTERS themselves (Y, Ω, D, M, Θ, L, Π) are the existing letter
library, drawn and recolored by the program — never new art here.

**REUSE — none.** Checked against the station files
([Two Crosses Prompts](crosses_prompts.md)) and the Prism emotion
seats: no candidate — the subjects differ by design (see the header).
