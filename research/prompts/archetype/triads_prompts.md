# Triads Prompts — the Trio's Stacked Readings Given Their Own Images

The abstract-trinity art set (owner-sealed 2026-07-22, RESTRUCTURE
§New Content). The Trinity pointer's three arms (12h · 20h · 04h)
accumulate READINGS the way the whole dial stacks canons — the Triads
(see [The DOMY Canon](../../../CANON.md), §The Triads). Until now those
readings lived only in the two-row articles; this sheet gives the
ABSTRACT trios — the ones that are concepts, not the seated persons —
their own images, per the new art doctrine (CANON §The abstract triads
& dualities art doctrine: **every stacked reading gets its own image**).

**The four trios** (all on the Trinity arm geometry — yellow 12h, red
20h, blue 04h — grounded in the CANON Triads table):

| Trio | 12h (yellow) | 20h (red) | 04h (blue) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Time** | the **Present** | the **Future** | the **Past** |
| **Callings** | the **Judge** | the **Prosecutor** | the **Advocate** |
| **Theological** | **Faith** | **Love** | **Hope** |
| **Dialectic** | **Synthesis** | **Antithesis** | **Thesis** |

Each concept rides the arm CANON already gives it: the clockwise arrow
of time (Past 04h → Present 12h → Future 20h); the court (Judge above,
the Prosecutor who strikes at the future in red, the Advocate who
defends deeds already done in blue); the theological trio (Faith with
the Word at the crown, Love-in-deed on the red future-arm, Hope the
medicine for the blue past-arm, Philippians 3:13); the dialectic
(Thesis = what stands / the past 04h, Antithesis = what challenges /
the future 20h, Synthesis = the judgment above / the present 12h —
today's verdict is tomorrow's precedent).

**Every figure is a CONCEPT, not a person** — the persons (God, the
Devil, Jesus; the Father, Mother, Child) already have their own lancets
in [Trinity Prompts](trinity_prompts.md) and
[Family Prompts](family_prompts.md). These windows draw the ABSTRACTION
itself (Time, the callings, the theological powers, the dialectic
turns), never a face.

**Register:** the house night-window stained-glass family — the SAME
register as the Trinity lancets and the Prism persons (see
[Persons Prompts](persons_prompts.md)). Each concept is TWO images:
a **TALL pointed-arch lancet** (aspect 1:2) in its arm's colour, AND a
**1:1 circle companion** that condenses the lancet's own headline scene
into a round badge — the badge system every archetype lancet now
carries (see [Badge 1:1 Prompts](../badge/badge_1to1_prompts.md)). The
circle keeps the lancet's exact palette and border-motif vocabulary so
the two read as one concept, two frames.

**Drop paths:** `assets/archetypes/triads/<trio>/<Figure>.png` (the
lancets) and `assets/archetypes/triads/<trio>/circle/<Figure>.png`
(the circle companions) — `<trio>` is `time`, `callings`, `theological`
or `dialectic`; sourceless (PromptPainter appends `_gem`/`_gpt`).

**House rules carried from every other sheet:** photorealistic render,
isolated background, the window shape IS the frame, NO lettering
anywhere in any image.
