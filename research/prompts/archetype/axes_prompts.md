**IRREDUCIBLE — generated (9 files).**

- **The One (1).** The centre of the Cube exists nowhere else in this
  project as an image, and it cannot be derived from anything: it is
  neither a figure (the centre takes no exemplar in any register, by
  doctrine) nor a blend of the six poles (it is not their average — it
  is the seat no force rules). One round rosette, no human form.
- **The Sacred Axis (2).** The five stations — Paralyzed Purist, Jesus,
  The One, Charismatic Champion, the Devil — in ONE composition, with
  the asymmetry the canon insists on: the luminous stations nearer the
  centre than the fallen ones. No arrangement of existing plates says
  that; it is a new scene. Plus its 1:1 circle companion, as every
  lancet family carries.
- **The six sacred figures (6).** Charter rule 5: three sets on one seat
  are three DIFFERENT PEOPLE holding one office. The principals and
  their historical and modern echoes are six different arguments and
  therefore six images. They are NOT variants of the Advocate, the
  Prosecutor or the Humility pole plates, which argue different seats of
  the same persons.

**COMPUTED — never generated.**

- **THE TWELVE HUMAN AXIS PAGES TAKE NO PLATE.** An axis is exactly its
  two ends through the centre; the Rose already draws every one of them
  by seating ray *k* against ray *k+12*. An "axis emblem" would be a
  composition of two windows that already exist, which is the definition
  of a derivable variant. *(PENDING OWNER: if the owner wants twelve
  standalone axis emblems in the manner of the Two Crosses' two path
  plates, it is a one-line decision and a new section here — it is not
  assumed, and nothing in the app waits on it.)*
- **THE HEXAGRAM PROJECTION IS NOT DRAWN.** The projection of a cube
  along its body diagonal is a hexagon with two triangles — the emblem
  this instrument has carried from the beginning, already on the glass
  and already rotating with true solar noon. Its two named X-rays are
  viewpoints, not pictures, and the Character-Cube model the 3D Preview
  gadget will read (WORKPLAN Session 28) computes both from the same
  canon data.
- **The 65 terms, the five stations' arithmetic, the 125 readings, the
  blindness count** — text and numbers. A table is not an asset.
- **Rotation, tint, opacity, the Diamond/Cube look** — transform and
  palette maths on the files below, as everywhere else in this project.

## Register and house rules

The house night-window stained-glass family, unchanged. Photorealistic
render, isolated background, the window shape IS the frame, at most ONE
contained glow source with every other light dim and matte, **NO
lettering anywhere** — no names, no monograms, no readable script of any
kind, in any language.

**Drop paths (the tree law, `assets/___assets.md`):**
`assets/archetypes/sacred/primary/colored/<Stem>.png` for the two seat
plates, `assets/archetypes/sacred/circle/colored/<Stem>.png` for the
companion, and `assets/archetypes/sacred/<set>/colored/<Stem>.png` for
the figures, where `<set>` is `archetypal`, `historical` or `modern`.
PromptPainter appends the `_gem`/`_gpt` source suffix.

**Copyright routing (Charter rule 7).** `_v1` named — public-domain,
historical, biblical and mythological figures, the name may stand inside
the fence. `_v2` descriptive — protected material: no name, no franchise
term, ever, inside a fence; the deed carries the plate. This sheet has
**two `_v2` entries and zero `_v3`**: both modern echoes proved drawable
from their own act without a likeness, so the owner supplies no
reference stills.

## The centre — The One

**The One — the seat no turn moves (the centre)** →
`assets/archetypes/sacred/primary/colored/The_One.png`

```
ROUND rose-window rosette, night-window stained-glass register, photorealistic render, isolated background, the circular window shape IS the frame. ASPECT RATIO exactly 1:1 — a perfect square image. NO FIGURE OF ANY KIND — no person, no face, no hand, no eye, no animal: the centre of this window is pure light. Center: a single small point of still, clear WHITE light at the exact middle, perfectly round and unmoving, with no rays, no flare and no lens effect. Around it, six equal petals of glass in a ring, each one leaning inward toward the white point and each a different colour — deep blue, burnt orange, radiant yellow, moon-gray violet, living green and ember red — every petal the same size and the same brightness, so that NONE dominates and none is dimmer than the others. Between the petals, six narrow slivers of plain clear glass. The outer ring of the rosette is dark tracery in tarnished silver, absolutely regular, twelve-fold. The white centre is the only light source in the image; the six coloured petals are lit BY it rather than glowing themselves. Palette: clear white light, deep blue, burnt orange, radiant yellow, moon-gray violet, living green, ember red, tarnished silver tracery. NO lettering anywhere.
```

*Why no figure: the centre takes no exemplar in any register (owner
verdict 2026-07-28) — every human example is ruled by something, and
this seat's whole content is that nothing rules it. Six equal petals ARE
the cataphatic description; the six clear slivers between them are the
apophatic one.*
