# Banknote Offices Prompts — the Fivefold Code of the Dollar Ring

The DOLLAR/EYE + CROSS-WORDS rounds (owner decrees 2026-07-27,
CANON.md §The Banknote — The Fivefold Offices): the Dollar ring's
five letters read as the Double Trinity's OFFICES, and the hover
legend names them — **M** Malignant Accuser, **A** Anointed Aegis,
**S** Satanic Scourge, **Ω** Omnific Originator, **N** Nazarene
Advocate. The sixth seat, the Eye of Providence (God as Judge),
already has its art — the four `Eye[_shine]_gem/_gpt` masters in the
ring letter library — and gets NO entry here (one image, one place).

The compressed formula the images serve: *the Accuser condemns, the
Aegis protects, the Scourge destroys, the Originator creates, the
Advocate defends — and the Judge watches.*

## THE HOUSE LETTERING RULE — read before generating

**NO lettering inside any image** — and on THIS family the rule needs
shouting, because real banknote engravings are covered in text:
no denominations, no mottos, no ANNUIT COEPTIS, no MASON letters, no
scrollwork words. The mottos already ring the dial as the ring's own
arc text; the plates carry only figure and ornament.
