## The colour law — read this before generating anything

CUBE.md seals it (owner 2026-07-26): **the palette stays exactly as the
Rose is drawn**, kept for the beauty of the arrangement. Two traps live
here and both have burned art rounds elsewhere in this project:

1. **The horizontal axis wears the SCALE's colours, not the Cube's.** On
   this wheel Humility is BLUE (06h) and Dignity is RED (18h) — Judas's
   midnight blue against Lucifer's ember red, the Judas–Lucifer axis as
   the Scale glass has always painted it. The Cube's own green/red
   Self-Regard dress binds the CUBE and its Encyclopedia section, NOT
   this wheel. A blue window here is not the Cube's blue (Composure).
2. **The four blends follow NATURE, not the Cube.** Green IS the child of
   blue and yellow (as on the Prism), orange of yellow and red; pink and
   cyan are red and blue THINNED BY THE MOONLIGHT of the neighbouring
   moon-purple pole — pale, cold-lit, silvered, never candy pink or
   swimming-pool cyan. Every prompt below states its own mixture in
   words so no generation has to infer it.

The same duality law that lets a great figure wear two colours closes the
circle here: Judas holds blue (his night) with green at his shoulder (his
fall of humility), Lucifer holds red (his pride) with orange at his
shoulder (his Destroyer face) — a colour may serve two wheels exactly as
a figure serves two offices.

**Register:** the house night-window stained-glass family. Each direction
is ONE **TALL pointed-arch lancet** (aspect 1:2) carrying BOTH readings —
the virtue in the upper light, its fall in the lower dark, one window,
two storeys (the [One Soul](one_soul_prompts.md) pillar-and-shadow
precedent) — plus a **1:1 circle companion** condensing the upper
reading (see [Badge 1:1 Prompts](../badge/badge_1to1_prompts.md)).

**Register & look (the tree law, `assets/___assets.md`):** every image
lands at `assets/archetypes/<family>/<register>/<look>/<Figure>[_vN]_<src>.png`
— the lancets in the **primary** register's **colored** look, the round
companions in the **circle** register's **colored** look. Sheets state
canonical SOURCE-LESS paths; PromptPainter appends the `_gem`/`_gpt`
suffix.

**Drop paths:** `assets/archetypes/character/primary/colored/` and
`assets/archetypes/character/circle/colored/`. Stems are the POSITIVE
direction's own name, PascalCase, no `_alt` anywhere: `Loyalty`,
`Patronage`, `Dignity`, `Conviction`, `Integrity`, `Renunciation`,
`Humility`, `Devotion`.

**No centre.** Both Compass archetypes carry `"center": None` in
`config.archetypes` — *the rose is the wheel itself* (owner). This wheel
inherits that; nothing is generated for the middle.

**House rules:** photorealistic render, isolated background, the window
shape IS the frame, **NO lettering anywhere** — the direction names, the
figures and the axis readings live in the articles and the legend.

**IRREDUCIBLE — generated (16 files):** eight DIRECTIONS that exist
nowhere else in the project as images — each a new composition holding a
virtue and its fall in one frame — plus their eight round companions.

  octa stars offset 15° (−1h / 0h / +1h), 24 rays in 8 colours, one ray
  per hour. That is one geometry drawn three times at three rotations
  with a palette lookup — a RING PRESET, the Mason ring's sibling. Not
  one pixel of it is art, and no future round may open a generation queue
  for it.
- **The eight wedge COLOURS**, including the four blends, are palette
  values computed in config (yellow+red → orange, blue+yellow → green,
  red/blue thinned toward moon-purple → pink/cyan). The wheel's coloured
  wedges are drawn, never painted plates.
- **The three SETS** (Historical −1h star, Modern 0h star, Archetypal
  +1h star) are a legend/roster READING of the same geometry — three
  rotations, not three art sets.
- **The missing depth axis** costs nothing to draw: Activation is absent
  by design, and absence is not an asset.
- **Rotation, lit/ghost opacity, metal tints, the Diamond/Cube toggle** —
  transform and palette maths on the same files, as everywhere else.

**DEFERRED — the per-figure portraits, PENDING OWNER.** CUBE.md §The
