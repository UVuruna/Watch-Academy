congregation of the mighty; he judgeth among the gods"* — the divine
council in session.

Preserver. The article layer CUBE.md holds in reserve reads the six
offices as the SIX DAYS of work and the centre as the seventh, the
Lord's Day rest; the centre below is drawn from that reading.

— lancets and the centre in the **primary** register's **colored** look,

## Rule #19 derivation check (written, root `CLAUDE.md` Rule #19)

The hard question this sheet exists to answer honestly: **six offices
that already have (or are getting) art on two other wheels — is a
Council image a NEW image at all?**

**COMPUTED — never generated:**

- **The interlock itself.** "Both triangles at once" is arm PLACEMENT:
  six files on six arms. No composite "star" image is ever generated.
- **The Diamond/Cube look toggle** (CUBE §Display laws) mounts the SAME
  files into corner-view rhombi — geometry, not a second art set.
- **Hexagram rotation, lit/ghost/centre-burn opacity, metal tints** —
  transform and palette maths, exactly as on every other wheel.
- **The 24-field union table** (12 outer offices, 12 inner processes) is
  Encyclopedia TEXT — twelve more office plates are not art we owe.
- **The Rose of the Twenty-Four gets NO sheet**: three identical octa
  stars at −15°/0°/+15°, 24 rays in 8 palette colours — computed
  geometry, a ring preset, never generated.

**IRREDUCIBLE — generated: the CENTRE, unconditionally (1 file).** The
Lord's Day rosette exists on no other wheel; the Court's Eye is the Court's
own union and cannot stand for the seventh day.

**THE SIX OFFICES — a genuine tension, referred to the owner.** Two laws
pull opposite ways here and this sheet refuses to resolve it silently:

- The **one-image-one-place law** (owner 2026-07-19, the law that revoked
  the Prism poles' reuse of the Scale glass) says every seat gets its own
  telling; a file serving two wheels is exactly what it forbade.
- **Rule #5 / Rule #19** say the same office by the same person in the
  same register, differing only by which arms its neighbours occupy, is
  not a new irreducible core.

The honest distinction, and the reason a new telling is defensible: on
the Court and Genesis wheels each office stands **alone and acting** — the
Advocate pleading, the Destroyer pulling the keystone. On the Council
every office is **in session**: seated in a ring of six thrones, turned
toward the shared centre, acting *before the others*. That is a different
COMPOSITION of the same person and office — the class of thing this
project does generate. Both routes are written out below; the owner picks
ONE.

- **Route A — SEALED (owner 2026-07-26, "može novo"):** six NEW
  "in session" lancets + six circles, 12 files, drop paths as stated.
- **Route B — RETIRED (the road not taken, kept for the record):**
  generate NOTHING but the centre;
  `config.archetypes["prism_council"]` points its six figures at the
  existing files —
  `archetypes/trinity/primary/colored/{One_Judge,Devil_Prosecutor,Jesus_Advocate}.png`
  and `archetypes/genesis/primary/colored/{God_Creator,Jesus_Preserver,Devil_Destroyer}.png`
  (and the matching `circle/colored/` companions). Technically free — the
  code reads a path, and nothing stops two archetype groups naming the
  same one. The Council then differs from Court + Genesis only in showing
  all six at once, which is arguably the entire point of a UNION wheel.

**Route A totals 13 files — the sealed source.** The route was sealed
2026-07-26; the old skip marker is lifted and all twelve entries load
ticked like any other queue.

## Route A — the six offices in session (SEALED 2026-07-26 — generate)

Each window shows its office AT THE COUNCIL TABLE: a ring of six carved
thrones is visible behind and to the sides, five of them occupied by dim
unreadable silhouettes in the other five arm colours, and the window's own
office lit and acting toward the shared centre. That ring is the visual
signature that separates a Council window from its Court or Genesis
sibling, and every prompt below states it.
