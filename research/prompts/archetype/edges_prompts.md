# Edge Cell Plates — the Eight New Edge Seats of the Cube

The eight EDGE cells the Thirteen-Axes round added to the canon
([The Cube Canon](../../../CUBE.md) §The 65 Terms, §The Rosters), each
holding a virtue and its fall. They are the Rose's own odd rays — the
seats between the six poles and the eight corners — and they are the
last cells of the twenty-six that had neither a wheel nor a plate.

**These plates are the SEAT, not the people on it.** No figure from the
roster is named and no recognisable person appears: every human form
below is anonymous — no identifiable face, no identifying costume, no
franchise object. The three figure SETS that ride these seats are the
separate sheet [Edge Figure Plates](../../rose_round/edge_figures_prompts.md).

is what makes it an edge rather than a pole or a corner, and it is the
one thing these eight windows have to teach that no other family teaches.

## The colour law — read this before generating anything

Three colours are in play in every window and they must never be mixed
up. The law is the [Vertex sheet](../../rose_round/vertices_prompts.md)'s,
adapted from three committed axes to two.

1. **The FIELD is the Rose's own arm hue** (`defaults.ROSE_PALETTE`,
   clockwise from 12h: yellow, orange, red, rose, purple-gray, cyan,
   blue, green) — the dominant glass of the whole window. **Rose is RED
   THINNED BY MOONLIGHT** — pale, cold, silvered, never candy pink;
   **cyan is BLUE THINNED BY MOONLIGHT** — pale, dusty, never neon;
   **purple-gray is moon-gray violet (~`#666699`)**, never royal.
2. **The two COMMITTED POLE COLOURS are the Cube's own dress** — blue
   and orange for Activation, yellow and purple-gray for Moral Scope,
   green and red for Self-Regard. They appear ONLY in the window's two
   devices, never as the field.
3. **THE THIRD PANE IS CLEAR.** The axis this seat holds at measure is
   drawn as PLAIN UNCOLOURED GLASS — the visible statement that an edge
   person is ruled by two things and free in the third. It is the one
   device this family owns and no other family has.

**The devices, identical on all eight lancets so the reader learns them
once:**

- **The apex trefoil** — three small panes in the lancet's pointed head:
  two in the committed pole colours, ONE CLEAR. Dim, matte, never a
  light source.
- **The two-strand braid** — the border leadwork is a braided cord of
  the two committed colours running the window's full height, with a
  third strand of plain silvered lead between them.
- **THE FALL IS ONE COMMITTED COLOUR SWALLOWING THE OTHER.** In the
  lower storey exactly one of the two has bled down over the whole glass
  and drowned its partner; the braid below runs single-stranded in that
  colour, the second strand cut, the clear strand gone dark. Which
  colour floods is derived from the seat's own written article and is
  stated in every prompt.

| Cell | Fall | Colour that floods | The article's own sentence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prudence | Indifference | **blue** (Composure → Lethargy) | "the deliberation kept up until the case decides itself" |
| Ardor | Vendetta | **yellow** (Loyalty → Tribalism) | "the wrong has replaced the person as the thing being served" |
| Steadfastness | Machination | **blue** (Composure → Lethargy) | "waits in the dark and arranges for somebody else to pay" |
| Reform | Zealotry | **purple-gray** (Integrity → Legalism) | "the rule enforced past every human case it was written to serve" |
| Meekness | Despair | **green** (Humility → Self-Annihilation) | "the lowering has reached the person himself" |
| Aspiration | Megalomania | **red** (Dignity → Self-Worship) | "the ceiling raised over other people rather than over the work" |
| Self-Mastery | Disdain | **red** (Dignity → Self-Worship) | "his standing is now held by contempt rather than by conduct" |
| Diligence | Servility | **green** (Humility → Self-Annihilation) | "the object changed from the task to the master" |

**Register:** the house night-window stained-glass family. Each seat is
ONE **TALL pointed-arch lancet** (aspect 1:2) carrying BOTH readings —
the virtue in the upper light, its fall in the lower dark, one window,
two storeys — plus a **1:1 circle companion** condensing the upper
reading, exactly as the Character and Vertex wheels are built.

**Drop paths (the tree law, `assets/___assets.md`):**
`assets/archetypes/edges/primary/colored/<Stem>.png` for the lancets and
`assets/archetypes/edges/circle/colored/<Stem>.png` for the companions.
Stems are the VIRTUE's own name, PascalCase: `Prudence`, `Ardor`,
`Steadfastness`, `Reform`, `Meekness`, `Aspiration`, `Self_Mastery`,
`Diligence`. PromptPainter appends the `_gem`/`_gpt` source suffix.

**No centre.** This family is not a wheel — it is eight seats of the
Rose and eight pages of the Encyclopedia. Nothing is generated for a
middle it does not have.

**House rules:** photorealistic render, isolated background, the window
shape IS the frame, at most ONE contained glow source with every other
light dim and matte, **NO lettering anywhere**.

## Rule #19 derivation check (written, root `CLAUDE.md` Rule #19)

**IRREDUCIBLE — generated (16 files):** eight SEATS that exist nowhere
else in this project as images — each a new composition holding a virtue
and its fall in one frame — plus their eight round companions. The
virtue/fall pair is deliberately ONE window and not two: a fall is the
same direction walked past its measure, and drawing it separately would
enumerate what one composition already says (the same argument the
Character wheel's sheet makes for its own sixteen files).

**COMPUTED — never generated:**

- **The Rose's geometry** — three identical stars at three offsets, 24
  rays in 8 palette colours — is drawn, never painted. Not one pixel of
  the wheel these seats ride is art.
- **The eight ray HUES**, including rose and cyan (red and blue thinned
  toward moon-purple), are palette values computed in config.
- **The seat's position, rotation and lit/ghost opacity**, the metal
  tints and the Diamond/Cube look — transform and palette maths on these
  same files.
- **The axis PAGES take no plate at all.** An axis IS its two ends
  through the centre: the twelve human axis pages of the Encyclopedia
  are rendered by placing the two end plates opposite one another, which
  is exactly what the Rose already does with them. A separate axis
  emblem would be a composition of two existing windows, and this rule
  forbids materializing that. *(PENDING OWNER — if the owner wants
  twelve standalone axis emblems in the manner of the Two Crosses' path
  plates, that is a one-line decision and a new section here; it is not
  assumed.)*
- **The three FIGURE SETS are NOT computed** — Charter rule 5: three
  sets on one seat are three DIFFERENT PEOPLE, so each is its own
  queued image. They are 48 files, in their own sheet.
