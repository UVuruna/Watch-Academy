# Genesis Archetype Prompts — the Creation Trio, the Triangle Turned Down

The TRINITY pointer's THIRD wheel (menu: Court / Family / **Genesis**),
sealed in [The Cube Canon](../../../CUBE.md) §The Double Trinity. The
same three persons who hold the Court's three JUDICIAL offices on the
upward triangle hold three CREATION offices on the opposite three arms
— and this wheel is drawn **INVERTED**, the triangle pointing DOWN
(owner: *"trougao ka dole"*).

| Arm | Colour | Person | Creation office | Article anchor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **24h** | moon-gray purple (~`#666699`) | **God** | **Creator** | Genesis 1:1 — *"In the beginning God created"* |
| **08h** | green | **Jesus** | **Preserver** | Colossians 1:17 — *"by him all things consist"* |
| **16h** | orange | **the Devil** | **Destroyer** | Job 1:12 — the power to unmake, granted and bounded |

**Judgment precedes Transformation** (CUBE §Double Trinity): the Court
decides what deserves to remain; Creation then makes, keeps and unmakes.
God holds the vertical axis alone — Judge at noon, Creator at midnight,
the same 12–24 diameter the Banknote reads as G↔Ω (Revelation 1:8). The
diagonals cross the persons: Advocate ↔ Destroyer, Prosecutor ↔
Preserver — defence against unmaking, accusation against keeping. That
crossing is the doctrinal reason each window here answers a Court window
on the opposite arm, and the prompts below are composed to be READ
against them.

**The colour of the 24h arm is NOT royal purple** (CUBE §The Purple-Gray
hue law, SEALED): it is the muted moon-gray violet of the Rose, ~`#666699`
— the Moon, midnight, the Winter Solstice. Every prompt on that arm says
so in its own words; a violet that reads as royal purple is a failed
generation, not a variation.

**Register:** the house night-window stained-glass family — the SAME
register as the Court's three lancets (see
[Trinity Prompts](trinity_prompts.md)) and the Scale glass (see
[Scale Prompts](../badge/scale_badge_prompts.md)). Each person is TWO
images: a **TALL pointed-arch lancet** (aspect 1:2) in the arm's colour,
AND a **1:1 circle companion** condensing the lancet's headline scene
into a round badge — the badge system every archetype lancet now carries
(see [Badge 1:1 Prompts](../badge/badge_1to1_prompts.md)). The circle
keeps the lancet's exact palette and border motif so the two read as one
office, two frames.

**Register & look (the tree law, `assets/___assets.md`, owner-approved
2026-07-26):** every image lands at
`assets/archetypes/<family>/<register>/<look>/<Figure>[_vN]_<src>.png` —
the lancets in the **primary** register's **colored** look, the round
companions in the **circle** register's **colored** look. Sheets state
canonical SOURCE-LESS paths; PromptPainter appends the terminal
`_gem`/`_gpt` suffix itself.

**Drop paths:** `assets/archetypes/genesis/primary/colored/` (lancets and
the centre) and `assets/archetypes/genesis/circle/colored/` (the round
companions). Stems are PascalCase and figure-first — `God_Creator`,
`Jesus_Preserver`, `Devil_Destroyer` — exactly like the Court's
`One_Judge` / `Jesus_Advocate` / `Devil_Prosecutor`; no `_alt` stem
exists anywhere in this project any more.

**House rules carried from every other sheet:** photorealistic render,
isolated background, the window shape IS the frame, and **NO lettering
anywhere in any image** — Genesis 1, the anchors above and the office
names live in the articles and the legend, never painted into the glass.

  (24h/16h/08h) — the renderer seats the same three files on the

  one geometry transform, zero files.
- **Lit / ghost / centre-burn states** — opacity math
  (`ARCHETYPE_CENTER_WINDOW_DEG`), not three brightness variants on disk.
- **Metal and tint variants** — the project derives gold/silver from a
  bronze master at load (`render.asset_recolor`); this family ships
  colored glass only, and no recolour of it is ever a file.
- **The 24-field union wheel** (12 outer offices × 12 inner processes,
  CUBE §Double Trinity) is a TABLE — Encyclopedia text, not 24 images.
- **The Rose of the Twenty-Four gets NO sheet at all**: three identical
  octa stars at −15°/0°/+15°, 24 rays in 8 palette colours — pure
  computed geometry, a ring preset, never art. (Stated in every sheet of

## The centre — the Beginning (name PROPOSED, PENDING OWNER)

The archetype engine gives every Trinity-family wheel a CENTRE
(`config.archetypes.ARCHETYPES[...]["center"]`): the Court has the Eye of
Providence, the Family has the Hearth. CUBE.md seals the Genesis wheel
but does not name its centre, so the NAME below is a proposal awaiting
the owner's word — the art itself is unambiguous, since it is simply the
Eye's own composition INVERTED, the same way the wheel is.

**PENDING OWNER — the name.** Proposed: **The Beginning**
(`Beginning.png`), anchored on Genesis 1:1, the verse CUBE.md already
gives this wheel. Alternatives the owner may prefer: *The Work*, *The
Deep*, *The First Day*. The file stem changes with the verdict; the
prompt does not.

**The Beginning** →
`assets/archetypes/genesis/primary/colored/Beginning.png`

```
ROUND rose window — a circular stained-glass rosette, night-window register, photorealistic render, isolated background. ASPECT RATIO exactly 1:1 — a perfect square image. Center: a large radiant TRIANGLE of glass POINTING DOWNWARD, its apex at the bottom of the window; the triangle's three SIDES are glazed in three colours — the upper horizontal side muted moon-gray violet (a cold dusty gray-violet, never royal purple), the left descending side living green, the right descending side burning orange, meeting cleanly at the corners. At its heart, where the Court's rosette carries an eye, this one carries the FIRST LIGHT over the deep: a small round pane of PERFECTLY CLEAR uncolored glass with plain white daylight passing through it, the brightest point of the window, and beneath it a dark still water that the light is only now touching. Straight rays of white glass burst from the triangle through rings of soft cloudglass to the rim. Rim: gothic stone tracery; three rim roundels evenly placed — a pair of open dividers in moon-gray violet glass, a cupped living sphere in green glass, a drawn keystone in orange glass. Palette: white first light, moon-gray violet, living green, burning orange, black-iron lead, the clear centre pane the only uncolored glass. NO lettering anywhere.
```

*(Read against `Providence_Eye.png`: the Court's triangle points UP and
holds an EYE that watches; Genesis's triangle points DOWN and holds the
LIGHT that makes. Same rosette grammar, opposite direction — which is the
whole doctrine of the Double Trinity in one pair of windows.)*
