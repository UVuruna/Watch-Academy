# Emotions Prompts — the Dozen of the Heart (System B)

The first of the four new Dozens (owner-sealed 2026-07-22, RESTRUCTURE
§New Content). **System B — the month-aligned wheel** (wedge centers ON
the cardinals, a crown, a root, and six opposition axes; see
[The DOMY Canon](../../../CANON.md), §The Two Dozen Systems): the
twelve emotions ride the twelve wedges, each sealed to its own hour.
Love crowns the wheel at noon; Hatred roots it at midnight — the same
Love–Hatred vertical the Prism primary Persons already argue, now spoken
as the naked emotion rather than the person who embodies it.

**The sealed hours (owner):** Love 12h, Hope 14h, Courage 16h,
Ambition 18h, Pride 20h, Envy 22h, Hatred 24h, Despair 02h, Fear 04h,
Doubt 06h, Humility 08h, Gratitude 10h. Six of the twelve — **Love,
Courage, Pride, Hatred, Fear, Humility** — are the Prism primary hexagram
itself (`One_Love`, `Michael_Courage`, `Lucifer_Pride`, `Devil_Hatred`,
`Judas_Fear`, `Jesus_Humility`), sitting here at their own prism hours;
the other six — **Hope, Ambition, Envy, Despair, Doubt, Gratitude** —
are the new intermediates that complete the Dozen.

**The six opposition axes** (System B's own geometry — every wedge
faces its opposite across the center): **Love 12h ↔ Hatred 24h**,
**Hope 14h ↔ Despair 02h**, **Courage 16h ↔ Fear 04h**,
**Ambition 18h ↔ Doubt 06h**, **Pride 20h ↔ Humility 08h**,
**Envy 22h ↔ Gratitude 10h**. Each emotion's cure stands directly
across the dial from it, exactly as the mood and virtue axes do in
[DOMY Symbolism](../../../SYMBOLISM.md): Fear is cured by Courage,
Despair by Hope, Hatred by Love, Doubt by Ambition (the will that acts
past hesitation), Pride by Humility, Envy by Gratitude (the answer that
looks at what one HAS instead of what another has).

**The colour wheel — a clean twelve-step ring.** The six prism-seat
emotions KEEP their exact prism hues (the color-fidelity notes below);
the six intermediates take the tertiary blend between their two
neighbours, so the ring reads as one unbroken spectrum turning:
yellow → amber → orange → ember → red → crimson → purple → indigo →
blue → teal → green → chartreuse, and back to yellow.

**Color fidelity across registers** (CANON, owner 2026-07-16): the six
prism-seat emotions do not MOVE colour here — they RETAIN it. This is
the cleanest case of the fidelity law: the same emotion, at the same
hour, under its own name instead of its person's. The italic notes
below state the retention for each of the six, exactly as the
[Persons Prompts](../archetype/persons_prompts.md) sheet states its
moves.

**Register:** the round calendar-mount medallion family — the SAME
night-window stained-glass rondel every 12-set that mounts on the
Calendar pointer already wears (see the
[Slavic Months Prompts](../months/months_prompts.md), the
[Calendar Prompts](../archetype/calendar_prompts.md)), so the Dozen of
the Heart sits beside the months and the signs without a style break.
ROUND rose-window medallions, one unifying device across all twelve:
**the Wheel of the Heart** — a leadwork rim of twelve small linked
hearts (one per emotion, the chain unbroken all the way round), the
family's own device the way the months wear the spoked cart-wheel and
the Almanac the wheel-and-tick. Each window's field is dominated by its
own emotion hue.

**Drop paths:** `assets/calendars/emotions/primary/colored/<Figure>.png` — a sourceless
calendar-mount root (PromptPainter appends `_gem`/`_gpt`). Twelve
files: `Love.png`, `Hope.png`, `Courage.png`, `Ambition.png`,
`Pride.png`, `Envy.png`, `Hatred.png`, `Despair.png`, `Fear.png`,
`Doubt.png`, `Humility.png`, `Gratitude.png`.

**House rules carried from every other sheet:** photorealistic render,
isolated background (no transparency-checkerboard artifact), the
circular window shape IS the frame, NO lettering anywhere in any image.
