# Sins Prompts — the Twelve Sins and the Frozen Heart (System B)

The fifth of the Dozens, and the deliberate counterpart of the Virtue
Wheel's PAINT register. **System B — the month-aligned wheel** (crown,
root, six opposition axes; see [The DOMY Canon](../../../CANON.md),
§The Two Dozen Systems), carrying the Christian tradition's catalogue
of **SIN** — Gregory the Great, Evagrius, Aquinas, Dante. The
distinction is the whole reason both wheels exist: a **vice** is
Aristotle's measure missed, too much or too little of a good thing; a
**sin** is a bond broken. The two traditions ride the same instrument
and are never merged.

**The twelve seats — SEALED by the owner 2026-07-29** (CANON §The Sins
Dozen; nothing here waits on a later verdict):

| Hour | Sin | Argument |
|---|---|---|
| **12h — crown** | **Pride** | Gregory's root RETURNS to the rim. He lifted pride off the seven and set it over them — *"radix quippe cuncti mali superbia est"* (*Moralia* XXXI) — and Vainglory existed on his list ONLY as the seat pride had vacated; the seal folds Vainglory back INTO Pride, one seat, the stronger word. *"Pride goeth before destruction"* (Proverbs 16:18) |
| 14h | Hypocrisy | the hour of the word about oneself (Truthfulness/Boastfulness on the Virtue Wheel): the settled lie, the *whited sepulchre* (Matthew 23:27) — *hypokrites* is Greek for stage-player, brightest at midday and hollow within |

Dante's ninth and deepest circle is the traitors' ice, Judas in Satan's own mouth (*Inferno* XXXIV), and the Apostles Dozen already seats Judas at 22–24h: the man and his sin end at the same midnight (John 13:18) |
| 02h | Despair | the indigo hour the Emotions give Despair — in the tradition a SIN, the sin against Hope (Aquinas, ST II-II q.20), the refusal of a mercy that was offered: Judas's second sin (Matthew 27:5) |

**The six axes:** 12↔24 **Pride ↔ Treachery**, the crown of SELF facing
the breaking of FAITH — love of self carried to its end IS betrayal of
the other; 14↔02 the lie told to OTHERS ↔ the lie believed about
ONESELF; 16↔04 fortitude's two acts corrupted, the ATTACK turned rabid
↔ the ENDURANCE snapped; 18↔06 the two false gods, the thing HOARDED ↔
the thing WORSHIPPED (*"covetousness, which is idolatry"*, Colossians
3:5 — Paul names this axis himself); 20↔08 the two appetites of the
flesh; 22↔10 Aquinas' two sorrows, sorrow at ANOTHER's good ↔ sorrow at
ONE'S OWN. The briefs below are unchanged by the seating — each figure
is argued by its own nature, hue following that nature.

**The 12h axis reads across THREE wheels** — Magnanimity (the virtue),
Vanity (its failing), Pride (the sin): one axis of self-regard, three
readings. Note the canon's own guard: the Emotions Dozen's **PONOS at
20h** is a POLE, not a vice — *the sin is only the tip past the clear
heart* — and Pride-the-sin is that tip, a different thing from the
healthy pole.

**Register:** the round calendar-mount medallion family — the same
night-window stained-glass rondel every Calendar-pointer 12-set wears
(see the [Slavic Months Prompts](../months/months_prompts.md), the
[Calendar Prompts](../archetype/calendar_prompts.md)). These thirteen
are cut in the PAINT register the Virtue Wheel's vices already use:
heavy, opaque, subtractive pigment that BLOCKS the light rather than
passing it. One unifying device across all thirteen: **the FROST
RING** — a rim of creeping ice-crystal frostwork in cold pale-blue and
grey-white leadwork, thickest at the rim and reaching inward with
needle-fine crystals. **Why frost:** this wheel does not end in fire,
it ends in ICE — Dante's bottom is Cocytus, the frozen lake, and this
Dozen's own axle is the heart that FROZE, so every window on the rim
carries the cold that finishes at its center; the frost tells a sin
from a virtue at a glance exactly the way the Mean's balance-beam tells
a virtue from its vice.

## The axle — the center that does not turn

**HARDNESS OF HEART** (*sklerokardia*) is the Sins Dozen's axle: always
present, never a rim seat (CANON §The Axle, §The Sins Dozen). It is the
**ANTI-PEACE** — the Emotions Dozen's center read the other way, and the
second of the two pairs the CENTER-PAIR LAW names: **Peace, the heart
still because it RESTS; Hardness of Heart, the heart still because it
FROZE.** Its anchors are one single scene: Jesus looks around *"being
grieved for the hardness of their hearts"* (Mark 3:5) and to those same
hardened hearts speaks the warning about the sin that *"hath never
forgiveness"* (Mark 3:29) — hardness is the MECHANISM of the
unforgivable, which is why **Blasphemy against the Spirit is ABSORBED
here rather than seated**. Pharaoh's hardened heart runs the same law
through Exodus. And the bottom of Dante's hell is ICE, not fire —
Cocytus, the frozen lake, with the TRAITORS in it: the Treachery root
at 24h stands directly above the frozen center it belongs to.
