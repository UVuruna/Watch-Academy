# Virtue Wheel Prompts — Aristotle's Twelve, in Two Registers (System B)

The second of the four new Dozens (owner-sealed 2026-07-22, RESTRUCTURE
§New Content). **System B — the month-aligned wheel** (crown, root, six
opposition axes; see [The DOMY Canon](../../../CANON.md), §The Two Dozen
Systems), carrying **Aristotle's twelve moral virtues** and their
twelve failing vices as **TWO REGISTERS OF ONE WHEEL** — the
paint/light doctrine made a Dozen. The LIGHT register draws the twelve
virtues; the PAINT register draws the twelve vices. Light is additive —
a virtue is made of the light that passes THROUGH the glass; paint is
subtractive — a vice is heavy pigment that blocks it. Same twelve
seats, two ways of reading the same wheel.

**The twelve pairs (Aristotle, Nicomachean Ethics II–IV):**
Courage/Cowardice, Temperance/Gluttony, Generosity/Greed,
Magnificence/Vulgarity, Magnanimity/Vanity, Patience/Wrath,
Truthfulness/Boastfulness, Wit/Buffoonery, Friendliness/Flattery,
Modesty/Shamelessness, Right Ambition/Over-ambition, Just
Indignation/Envy. Each virtue is a MEAN; each vice its extreme.

**Register:** the round calendar-mount medallion family — the same
night-window stained-glass rondel every Calendar-pointer 12-set wears
(see the [Slavic Months Prompts](../months/months_prompts.md), the
[Calendar Prompts](../archetype/calendar_prompts.md)). ROUND rose-window
medallions, one unifying device across all twenty-four: **the Mean** —
a slender balance-beam leadwork rim laid across the top of the disc. On
a VIRTUE the beam hangs LEVEL and true, cast in bright clear lead; on a
VICE the beam is TILTED hard to one side (its own excess or deficiency)
and cast in tarnished, corroded lead. The border itself tells virtue
from vice at a glance, the way the Prism-light pillars carry their own
shadow.

**Drop paths:** `assets/calendars/virtues/<Figure>.png` (the twelve
LIGHT windows) and `assets/calendars/vices/<Figure>.png` (the twelve
PAINT windows) — sourceless calendar-mount roots (PromptPainter appends
`_gem`/`_gpt`). Stems are the virtue/vice English names:
`Courage.png` … and `Cowardice.png` …. Figure-first: the seat a figure
holds on the wheel lives in the roster and this sheet's prose, never in
the filename.

**House rules carried from every other sheet:** photorealistic render,
isolated background (no transparency-checkerboard artifact), the
circular window shape IS the frame, NO lettering anywhere in any image.

## Status — the crown/root layout — PENDING OWNER APPROVAL

The Dozen's geometry (System B) is sealed; the SEAT ORDER around the
wheel is a **PROPOSAL awaiting the owner's verdict**. The default the
round brief named is **Courage crown / Cowardice root** — and the
cleanest way to honour it, and the one proposed here, is the
**paint-is-light-inverted-through-the-center** reading:

> The LIGHT wheel seats the twelve virtues; the PAINT wheel is the SAME
> wheel read as its own shadow, so **every virtue's vice sits
> diametrically opposite its virtue** — the shadow falling at the far
> pole. Courage crowns the light at 12h; its vice Cowardice therefore
> roots the paint at 24h, directly beneath the crowning virtue. This is
> the paint/light doctrine made geometric: light rises to the crown,
> its darkness sinks to the opposite root.

Proposed seating (LIGHT register, clockwise from the crown):

| Hour | Virtue (light) | Vice at this hour (paint) |
|---|---|---|
| 12h (crown) | **Courage** | Boastfulness |
| 14h | Temperance | Buffoonery |
| 16h | Generosity | Flattery |
| 18h | Magnificence | Shamelessness |
| 20h | Magnanimity | Over-ambition |
| 22h | Patience | Envy |
| 24h (root) | Truthfulness | **Cowardice** |
| 02h | Wit | Gluttony |
| 04h | Friendliness | Greed |
| 06h | Modesty | Vulgarity |
| 08h | Right Ambition | Vanity |
| 10h | Just Indignation | Wrath |

The paint column simply lists, at each hour, the vice of the virtue
seated OPPOSITE it — so the two registers occupy one wheel, twelve
seats, read two ways. Two happy consequences fall out for free:
**Truthfulness roots the light wheel while its own lie, Boastfulness,
crowns the paint wheel** (the truth at the bottom of the day, the boast
at the top of the shadow); and the crown-root vertical carries
**Courage over Cowardice** in each register's own reading.

**If the owner prefers the simpler alternative** — virtue and vice at
the SAME seat (Courage AND Cowardice both at 12h, the two registers
perfectly co-located) — the briefs above need NO change; only this
seating table would flip the paint column to match the light column.
The briefs are written seat-agnostic (each figure argued by its own
nature, hue following that nature, never a fixed spectral step), so
either layout ships the same twenty-four windows.
