The Rose's PROPHECY wheel (the Cube's eight vertices, seated as a
Hamiltonian cycle in [The Cube Canon](../../CUBE.md) §Seating the eight

**Drop path:** `assets/archetypes/vertices/<register>/colored/<Stem>.png`

---

## The law this sheet obeys — Charter rule 6, the DEED DOCTRINE

**A figure on a seat is not a verdict on a life. It is ONE DEED, lifted
out and held up as the symbol of that seat.** Everything else about the
person is deliberately forgotten. The owner's own example: Stalin is
seated on steadfastness by the refusal to trade his captured son and by
nothing else — the seat does not claim the man was steadfast, it claims
THAT ACT was.

Three consequences govern every prompt below:

1. **The plate depicts the ACT, never a portrait.** No badge below is "a
   picture of X". Each is a moment: what was done, under what pressure,
   at what cost.
2. **Each deed is the audit's deed, not the article's** where the two
   differ. Several sealed articles in `symbolism.json` lean on a
   duration ("seventy years"), a career ("the decades of Ranger
   patrol"), a pattern ("the hardening after each plague") or an
   apocryphal quotation — Charter rule 6 rejects all four in its own
   words. Every such seat carries an italic CORRECTION note above its
   prompt, and the prompt draws the corrected act.
3. **The act is also the copyright door.** We are never drawing a
   protected character's likeness; we are drawing a moment. That is why
   fourteen protected figures below sit safely at route `_v2`
   (descriptive, no name, no franchise term inside the fence) instead of
   needing a reference still.

## Register and house rules

The house night-window stained-glass family, badge form: every plate is
ONE **ROUND 1:1 stained-glass badge medallion**, photorealistic render,
isolated background, the circular window shape IS the frame — the same
register as the Character wheel's round companions
([Character Archetype Prompts](../prompts/archetype/character_prompts.md)).

**The colour law.** A vertex is a COMBINATION of three axis poles, so
its glass carries the **blend of its three axis colours** — X Activation
(blue Composure ↔ orange Vigor), Y Judgment (yellow Loyalty ↔
purple-gray Integrity), Z Self-Regard (green Humility ↔ red Dignity).
The LEADWORK takes the arm's own hue on the dial. Every prompt states
its own mixture in words so nothing has to be inferred.
