# Prophecy Wheel Figure Arguments — the Eight Cube Vertices, Three Sets, Both Polarities

Forty-eight figure arguments for the Rose's PROPHECY wheel (the Cube's
eight vertices, seated as a Hamiltonian cycle in
[The Cube Canon](../../CUBE.md)). Each entry argues ONE DEED under
Charter rule 6: what was done, under what pressure, at what cost, and
why that shape IS this seat. The person's name is the label on the deed,
never the thesis.

**Key scheme.** `<vertex_archetype_snake>_<register>` — the sixteen
vertex archetype names (positive and fallen) crossed with the three
registers `archetypal | historical | modern`. The seat's own article
(the vertex as such) already lives in `articles.archetype_rose_vertices`
and is untouched.

**Flag lines.** Where the deed audit returned *weak* or *replace*, or
where the sealed seat article runs a deed Charter rule 6 rejects
(a duration, a career, a pattern, an apocryphal quotation, a film-only
invention), the entry closes with an italic line stating the difficulty
plainly. The canon carries a flagged seat rather than a dishonest
argument.

---

## quiet_devotee_archetypal

The consent at the annunciation holds this corner: Mary answers an
unverifiable summons with one unconditional sentence — *"Behold the
handmaid of the Lord; be it unto me according to thy word"* (Luke 1:38)
— and attaches nothing to it. The pressure is not sentimental but legal:
under her own law a betrothed woman found pregnant was exposed to
stoning (Deuteronomy 22:23–24), and the cost is neither bargained down
nor even named by her. That is the vertex's exact arithmetic. Composure,
because nothing is set in motion — no argument, no condition, no request
for a sign, where Zacharias in the same chapter asks for one and is
silenced. Loyalty, because a bond is accepted whole before any of its
consequences are known. Humility, because not one clause of the answer
concerns her own standing. The yellow arm crowns the seat for the reason
the wheel gives it: a covenant kept quietly is the oldest form judgment
takes. The fall of this corner — the Submissive Enabler — is separated
from her by grammar alone: she answers in the first person rather than
being disposed of. Facing her at 24h, Joseph applies a rule where she
accepts a bond; the two are the wheel's clearest statement that judgment
has two grounds.

> *"Speak; for thy servant heareth."* (1 Samuel 3:10)

## quiet_devotee_historical

The deed is the return from Crimea in August 1856, made under the name
"Miss Smith". A national reception was prepared, bands and a warship
were offered by a grateful government, and Florence Nightingale declined
the whole apparatus, travelled alone, and arrived at Lea Hurst
unannounced. The work was already done; what the act refuses is the
payment for it. This is the vertex and not merely modesty, because all
three axes are visible at once in a single decision: Composure, in that
nothing is set in motion and no statement is issued; Loyalty, in that
the service itself continues undiminished afterward; Humility, in that
the reward is declined at the one moment it was on offer and would have
been universally granted. The seat's signature is that its work is
almost never visible while it is being done, and a refused triumph is
the purest available proof of it. Across the wheel at 24h, George
Washington declines power where she declines applause — the same act on
two different currencies.

> *"Let not thy left hand know what thy right hand doeth... and thy
> Father which seeth in secret himself shall reward thee openly."*
> (Matthew 6:3–4)

*Flag: the sealed seat article seats her on the mortality statistics and
the Royal Commission. That deed is high-activation, rule-based advocacy
pressed against an institution — the Principled Reformer's corner at
06h — and it argues the wrong vertex. The incognito return is the deed
with this vertex's shape.*

## quiet_devotee_modern

Faramir's seat is one refusal, spoken at Henneth Annûn: *"I would not
take this thing, if it lay by the highway. Not were Minas Tirith falling
in ruin and I alone could save her."* The pressure is maximal and
precisely targeted — the object offers exactly the power his city needs
and his brother reached for, and the law of his own captaincy would have
justified the seizure. The cost is that he lets the errand walk out of
his territory with nothing secured and no credit taken. What makes it
this vertex rather than the Reformer's is the discriminator that follows:
he never afterwards offers the refusal as a claim on anyone, and no
witness whose opinion matters is present to record it. Composure — he
does not struggle with the thing, he simply declines the office it
confers. Loyalty — to an errand and a stranger, over his own city's
advantage. Humility — the standing it would have won him against his
father's preference is the very thing set down. At 24h he faces Albus
Dumbledore: an office declined by a captain nobody was watching, and one
declined by a man an entire state was asking.

> *"He that is slow to anger is better than the mighty; and he that
> ruleth his spirit than he that taketh a city."* (Proverbs 16:32)

*Flag: this deed exists in the BOOK only. The film reverses it and
marches the errand to Osgiliath; the article and its art take Tolkien's
version and not the screen's.*

## submissive_enabler_archetypal

Ophelia is seated by the lie in the lobby — asked where her father is,
with Polonius listening, she answers *"At home, my lord."* The act is
small, checkable and complete: she takes the place assigned to her as a
decoy, returns the gifts on instruction, and deceives the man she loves
in the service of the man who commands her. The fall of this corner is
not disloyalty; it is the vanishing of the person doing the loving, and
the lie marks the exact instant she is no longer present inside her own
devotion. Two bonds pull against each other and, rather than choose
between them, she vacates the position from which a choice could be
made. That is why the fall wears the shape of the virtue from outside:
every axis still points away from the self, but now there is no self
left for them to point away from. Humility without a floor under it is
not humility but absence. Facing her at 24h, the Grand Inquisitor
dissolves everyone else into his judgment; she dissolves herself into
another's.

> *"For do I now persuade men, or God? or do I seek to please men? for
> if I yet pleased men, I should not be the servant of Christ."*
> (Galatians 1:10)

## submissive_enabler_historical

The deed is the journey into the encircled capital in April 1945: Eva
Braun was ordered to remain in Bavaria, travelled to Berlin instead,
entered the bunker, refused every subsequent chance to leave, married on
29 April and died on 30 April. It is dated, nameable and entirely
voluntary — the pressure ran the other way, and the order she disobeyed
was an order to live. The seat is held because the fidelity is genuine
and its object is the worst available: this is the doctrine at full
strength, a deed argued without any verdict on the person and without
any need for one. What has failed is not loyalty but the question
loyalty is supposed to keep asking — what is this for — and the failure
is invisible from outside precisely because the conduct is
indistinguishable from the virtue. Loyalty, Composure and Humility all
hold their positive form; only the floor beneath them is gone. At 24h
she faces Metternich, and the pair states the corner's whole range: the
person disappears by decree in his case and by consent in hers.

> *"There is a way which seemeth right unto a man, but the end thereof
> are the ways of death."* (Proverbs 14:12)

## submissive_enabler_modern

Peter Pettigrew's seat is the severing of his own right hand into the
cauldron — *"flesh of the servant, willingly given."* The ritual's own
wording carries the argument: the ingredient is invalid unless it is
volunteered, and it is volunteered. Nothing extracts it; he supplies it,
weeping, and does not stop. This is loyalty overdone rather than loyalty
betrayed, which is the only thing that can seat this corner. Service so
complete that the servant dismembers himself so that the thing he serves
can exist is the keeper disappearing into the keeping, and the missing
axis is the one that would have asked what is being enabled. Facing him
at 24h, Saruman hands himself to a greater power by calculation; Peter
hands over a piece of his body by obedience, and the two together mark
the difference between a fallen judgment and an absent one.

> *"Know ye not, that to whom ye yield yourselves servants to obey, his
> servants ye are to whom ye obey?"* (Romans 6:16)

*Flag: the sealed seat article seats him on the sale of the Potters'
hiding place. Betrayal is the opposite of loyalty, not loyalty carried
past its measure, and therefore argues no part of this vertex. The
graveyard deed is the seat.*

## sacrificial_protector_archetypal

Moses is seated by a single sentence spoken after the golden calf:
offered a nation made of his own descendants in place of the one that
had just disowned him (Exodus 32:10), he answers *"if not, blot me, I
pray thee, out of thy book which thou hast written"* (Exodus 32:32). The
offer is promotion; the answer is erasure. Every axis of the vertex is
inside that one clause. Vigor, because intercession is an act taken
against a verdict already pronounced, not a wait. Loyalty, because the
bond is honoured at the exact moment it has been forfeited by the other
side. Humility, because the protector asks to be subtracted rather than
raised — he does not petition for a share of the outcome, he petitions
to be absent from it. One axis separates this corner from the Quiet
Devotee, Activation, and it is the whole difference between keeping
faith and fighting for it. Across the wheel at 03h, Aeneas leaves
himself out of a founding while Moses leaves himself out of a rescue.

> *"Greater love hath no man than this, that a man lay down his life for
> his friends."* (John 15:13)

*Flag: Moses holds a seat on the LEGACY wheel too. The audit resolves
the collision by moving Legacy's Devotion to Ruth, so this act and its
one image stay here — but the double-seating of the man across two
wheels is the owner's to confirm.*

## sacrificial_protector_historical

The deed is the December 1854 return into Maryland, when Harriet Tubman
brought her brothers Ben, Henry and Robert out of Poplar Neck on
Christmas Eve. The argument turns entirely on the direction of travel:
the escape is survival and seats nobody, while the re-entry is a danger
already known, priced and chosen again — roughly thirteen times, by a
woman who was already free and already advertised, into a state that
would have hanged her. Nothing was recoverable by it that she did not
already possess, which removes every reading except the vertex's own.
Vigor, because each return is a planned operation and not an endurance.
Loyalty, because the object is particular people rather than a cause.
Humility, because the rescuer never once occupies the safety she
manufactures for others. Facing her at 03h, Atatürk spends a position
already won by abolishing the office; she spends hers by walking back
into the country it exempted her from.

> *"If thou forbear to deliver them that are drawn unto death, and those
> that are ready to be slain."* (Proverbs 24:11)

## sacrificial_protector_modern

Harry Potter's seat is the walk into the Forbidden Forest, unarmed, with
the wand never raised. The deed must be argued under the belief he held
while performing it — that the death was final — because that belief is
what makes it admissible: the later survival is a different act on a
different seat and does not reach back to cheapen this one. Under that
belief the act contains no bargain, no combat and no expectation. It is
the vertex's discriminator in its purest available form: the protector
deliberately declines to occupy the place he is protecting. Vigor, in
that he goes rather than waits to be found; Loyalty, in that the object
is a specific set of people still fighting; Humility, in that the whole
transaction is designed to end with him absent from its benefit. Facing
him at 03h is the Visionary Founder — the same energy spent on building
a thing rather than on standing between people and a thing.

> *"He was oppressed, and he was afflicted, yet he opened not his
> mouth."* (Isaiah 53:7)

## fanatical_martyr_archetypal

Samson's seat is Gaza: blind, he takes hold of the two pillars and asks
*"Let me die with the Philistines"* (Judges 16:30). The text supplies
its own discriminator two verses earlier, where he asks to be avenged
*"for my two eyes"* (16:28) — the stated object of the deed is his own
injury, not his people's deliverance, and no one is being rescued by it.
That is the fall exactly: protection that has fallen in love with its
own cost, where the payment has become the justification and the thing
protected has become secondary. Vigor is intact and enormous; Loyalty
survives in form, aimed at a bond that no longer requires this; Humility
has inverted into self-expenditure, which is not the absence of
self-regard but its most expensive assertion. Across the wheel at 03h,
Pharaoh drives an army into a sea for the same reason in a different
office — one destroys himself to prove the bond, the other destroys his
force to prove himself.

> *"Be not righteous over much; neither make thyself over wise: why
> shouldest thou destroy thyself?"* (Ecclesiastes 7:16)

*Flag: Samson holds a seat on the LEGACY wheel too, on this same act.
The audit resolves it by moving Legacy's Martyrdom to Dido, leaving Gaza
to this wheel — again a double-seating of the man that the owner should
confirm.*

## fanatical_martyr_historical

On 25 November 1970 Yukio Mishima and four Tatenokai cadets seized the
commandant's office at Ichigaya, addressed the assembled Self-Defence
Force from the balcony, were jeered down, and he committed seppuku. The
deed is dated, witnessed and complete, and it fails the protector's test
in the only way that matters: apply the vertex's own instrument — remove
the audience — and nothing of the act survives. A garrison assembled
under duress is not a constituency, and the thing said to be protected,
the nation's spirit, was never a party to the transaction; only the
payment was real. Vigor is undeniable and Loyalty is present in the
form of an allegiance to an idea rather than to any person who could
consent to it, while Humility inverts into a demand to be seen paying.
Facing him at 03h, Napoleon makes his own body the argument by crowning
it; Mishima makes his the argument by opening it.

> *"They love to pray standing in the synagogues and in the corners of
> the streets, that they may be seen of men. Verily I say unto you, They
> have their reward."* (Matthew 6:5)

## fanatical_martyr_modern

Kylo Ren's seat is the killing on the Starkiller bridge, and the seat's
whole content is the reason he gives for it: he states in advance that
the act is tearing him apart and performs it because the tearing is
supposed to complete his commitment. That is the fall's mechanism in
plain form — payment offered as proof, cost mistaken for consecration —
and the vertex's Vigor and Humility-inverted-into-self-expenditure are
both genuinely present in the deed. Facing him at 03h, Paul Atreides
takes a throne over a counted graveyard; the two are the apprentice who
pays and the master who spends.

> *"For what shall it profit a man, if he shall gain the whole world,
> and lose his own soul?"* (Mark 8:36)

*Flag (audit: WEAK). The person destroyed is SOMEONE ELSE, which makes
this a betrayal-deed dressed as sacrifice; the self-cost is real but
secondary, and the seat then leans on the character's general arc rather
than on the act, drifting toward the Messianic Tyrant's apprenticeship.
The proposed replacement is Nux — mouth sprayed chrome, "I live, I die,
I live again", launched at the war rig to die witnessed for a warlord: a
glorious death that IS the entire object, protecting nothing, with the
audience built into the deed.*

## charismatic_champion_archetypal

Beowulf is seated by the disarming before Grendel: he lays aside sword
and mail-shirt and orders his companions to hold back, on the stated
ground that the creature carries no weapon and knows no war-craft. What
makes this the positive side of a dangerous arm is that fairness is
extended past every point where it could have been demanded — no one in
the hall asks it, the enemy cannot appreciate it, and the concession has
no witness who could enforce it later. The champion raises the standard
against himself rather than lowering it against an opponent, which is
the exact difference between Dignity and Self-Worship on this axis.
Vigor: he takes the fight and does not wait for it. Loyalty: the hall is
not his own, and he is discharging a debt of his people's, not defending
property. Dignity: his own worth is the stake, wagered openly and priced
by the terms he sets. Facing him at 06h, Antigone raises an older
standard against a decree in private where he raises one in front of
everyone.

> *"The magnanimous man claims what is in accordance with his merits."*
> (Aristotle, *Nicomachean Ethics* IV.3)

## charismatic_champion_historical

The deed is the refusal of 28 May 1940: against Halifax's push in the
War Cabinet to let Mussolini explore terms, Churchill refused to open
any negotiation while the army was still on the beaches, and put the
same position to the Outer Cabinet that afternoon. The pressure is
institutional and lethal to him personally — the men in the room could
have overruled and replaced him, the case for terms was respectable, and
the judgment he staked a nation on was one he could not demonstrate.
This is the vertex rather than mere defiance because the decision was
made where it could be answered for: Vigor in that it is a decision and
not a mood, Loyalty in that its object is the thing led rather than the
man leading, and Dignity in the willingness to be the visible author of
an unprovable call. At 06h he faces Martin Luther King Jr., and the pair
frames the arm: both argue to their own side, one from the head of a
cabinet table and one from a cell.

> *"The secret of happiness is freedom, and the secret of freedom is
> courage."* (Pericles, in Thucydides II.43)

## charismatic_champion_modern

Wonder Woman's seat is the crossing at Veld: she climbs out of the
trench alone and walks upright across open ground, drawing the fire that
had frozen a line for a year so that the men can follow. Visibility here
is not vanity but mechanism — the deed only works because she is seen,
by the guns that must be pulled onto her and by the soldiers who must
watch it be survivable. That is what distinguishes this corner from the
Sacrificial Protector one arm back: the protector subtracts himself, the
champion must be present and known. Vigor in the advance, Loyalty
extended to people who are not hers and owe her nothing, Dignity in the
open claim of a capacity she actually has. Facing her at 06h, Luke
Skywalker proves worth by what he discards where she proves it by what
she will not hide.

> *"The wicked flee when no man pursueth: but the righteous are bold as
> a lion."* (Proverbs 28:1)

## tribal_warlord_archetypal

Agamemnon is seated by the taking of Briseis in *Iliad* I: compelled to
give up Chryseis, he removes another man's prize to keep his own honour
whole, and the army he commands pays for it in weeks of dead. The deed
is argued in his own terms and not in a critic's — he states the matter
as one of rank, that the supreme commander's portion cannot be less than
another's, and he is neither deceived nor beyond reason while saying it.
That is the fall precisely: the tribe stops being what the champion
serves and becomes what proves him, with the entire cost transferred to
the people the rank exists for. Vigor and Loyalty remain in place, but
the bond now runs one way; Dignity has become a quantity to be kept
level rather than a worth to be answered for. Facing him at 06h, Claude
Frollo converts an office into a claim on a person — the same conversion
in a smaller jurisdiction.

> *"Ye know that the princes of the Gentiles exercise dominion over
> them... but it shall not be so among you."* (Matthew 20:25–26)

*Flag: the sealed seat article takes Aulis. The sacrifice of Iphigenia
is a trade with a god and belongs to a different argument; Briseis is
the deed that is this arm.*

## tribal_warlord_historical

The deed is 28 October 1922. The squadristi columns converged on Rome
while Mussolini waited in Milan, the demonstration itself was accepted
as title to govern, and he arrived afterwards by sleeper train to be
appointed. Nothing was won by argument and nothing by battle; what was
offered and accepted was the roar of one's own. The fall lives in that
substitution — the crowd is not consulted, it is exhibited, and its
noise is the only measure the transaction admits. Vigor is real in the
movement, Loyalty runs strictly inward to the tribe that is his, and
Dignity has decayed into a name to be acclaimed rather than a worth to
be answered for. Facing him at 06h, Savonarola also moves a city with
his own voice, but toward the burning of its goods on a standard rather
than toward the installation of a name.

> *"Thou shalt not follow a multitude to do evil."* (Exodus 23:2)

## tribal_warlord_modern

Khal Drogo's seat is the oath sworn before the assembled khalasar after
the attempt on his wife: he vows to take the horde across the poison
water and put his son on the Iron Throne. The trigger is a genuine bond
— the deed cannot be argued without it — and the response converts that
bond into a licence. This is the fall's exact motion: the bond is not
served by the vow, it is spent as currency for the leader's honour, and
the followers' devotion becomes the fuel that pays for it. One nameable
act, sworn aloud in front of the people who will settle the account.
Vigor and Loyalty intact, Dignity swollen into the only measure the vow
admits. Facing him at 06h, Stannis Baratheon also spends his own
household on a claim; the zealot does it by a correct rule, the warlord
by a public oath.

> *"From whence come wars and fightings among you? come they not hence,
> even of your lusts that war in your members?"* (James 4:1)

## steady_guardian_archetypal

Hector is seated by the decision to stay outside the Scaean gate. The
knowledge is complete — he has watched what Achilles does, Priam and
Hecuba are begging from the wall, the Trojans are already inside, and
the postern is open beside him. Nothing about the choice is impulsive or
uninformed, which is why it is this corner and not the Champion's one
arm back: the Activation axis turns down, and the guardian's entire
strength is that he does not move. A post kept because it is the post,
with the outcome known and the exit available, is the vertex stated
without remainder. Composure and Dignity are both inside the standing
still — the first because the decision costs no argument, the second
because the worth being defended is his own office and he does not
pretend otherwise — and Loyalty names the city he holds the ground for. Facing him
at 09h, Job holds a position by reasoning where Hector holds one by
rank; both refuse the door that is standing open.

> *"Take unto you the whole armour of God... and having done all, to
> stand."* (Ephesians 6:13)

## steady_guardian_historical

The deed is the Cape Town broadcast of 21 April 1947: on her
twenty-first birthday, before she held the post, Elizabeth II declared
*"that my whole life, whether it be long or short, shall be devoted to
your service."* One dated act, whose entire content is the acceptance of
a position — made in public, in binding terms, and made early enough
that it could not be a description of anything already done. The
seventy years that followed are then evidence for the deed rather than
the deed itself, which is what the seat requires: Composure in that the
vow commits to no programme and asks for no latitude, Loyalty in the
object, Dignity in that the undertaking is made in the first person and
never afterwards renegotiated in public. Facing her at 09h, Tolstoy
likewise binds himself by an instrument published for anyone to hold him
to, and pays for it afterwards.

> *"When thou vowest a vow unto God, defer not to pay it... better is it
> that thou shouldest not vow, than that thou shouldest vow and not
> pay."* (Ecclesiastes 5:4–5)

*Flag: the sealed seat article seats her on "the length of the thing
itself — seventy years". A duration is not an act, and Charter rule 6
rejects it in its own words. The Cape Town vow is the deed; the length
is its evidence.*

## steady_guardian_modern

Aragorn's seat is the refusal at the walls after the Pelennor: he furls
his standard, sets the claim aside, and camps outside Minas Tirith
rather than press a kingship on a city still at war under a divided
stewardship. The right is real, enforceable and acknowledged by the men
who matter, and it is simply not exercised. That is the vertex rather
than modesty, because the axis being held is Dignity and not Humility —
he does not deny the claim, dispute it or hand it away; he declines to
collect on it at a moment when collecting would cost the city something.
Composure supplies the waiting, Loyalty fixes the object as the city
rather than the claim upon it, and the arm's rose — red thinned by
moonlight — is exactly this: dignity that waits. Facing him at 09h,
Gandalf does the decisive work where nobody is watching; the two are one
argument about credit deferred.

> *"Put not forth thyself in the presence of the king... For better it
> is that it be said unto thee, Come up hither."* (Proverbs 25:6–7)

*Flag: the sealed seat article seats him on "the decades of Ranger
patrol". A career is not an act. The deed is the refusal at the walls,
and it is Tolkien's, not the film's.*

## complacent_nepotist_archetypal

King Lear is seated by the first scene: a kingdom apportioned by a
love-test, with the map already drawn. The detail that makes it this
fall rather than simple misjudgment is that the division is fixed in
advance and only the praise is variable — government has become the
distribution of a reward against affection, and the daughter who
declines to bid is disinherited on the spot for declining. Composure and
Dignity are entirely intact; what has gone is the vigilance the post
exists for. The stillness of the Steady Guardian has stopped being a
watch and become a chair, and the office is administered as a household
matter with the state as its estate. Facing him at 09h, Hamlet refuses
to act on a scruple where Lear acts on a flattery; both have replaced
the standard the office requires with a private one.

> *"A man that flattereth his neighbour spreadeth a net for his feet."*
> (Proverbs 29:5)

*Flag: Lear holds Favoritism on the LEGACY wheel, and this is the only
Lear act of the required weight. The audit resolves the collision by
moving Legacy to Jacob (the coat of many colours), leaving the deed and
its one image here.*

## complacent_nepotist_historical

Louis XV is seated by the disgrace of Maurepas on 24 April 1749: his
naval and household minister, one of the few competent administrators in
the government, was dismissed and exiled over satirical verses touching
Madame de Pompadour, and the direction of ministerial appointment passed
into a favourite's protection. The act is dated and exactly this fall —
a state office spent on a household grievance, with the removal falling
on a man whose competence was the very thing the ministry lacked.
Composure and Dignity survive as manners; the watch is handed over
without ever being resigned. Facing him at 09h, Nicholas II holds an
inheritance without examining it where Louis spends an office without
tending it — the same abdication by two routes.

> *"Woe to thee, O land, when thy king is a child, and thy princes eat
> in the morning."* (Ecclesiastes 10:16)

*Flag (audit: WEAK). The canonized deed — "après moi, le déluge" — is
apocryphal and is usually attributed to Pompadour ("après nous"); it
must not be used. The Maurepas disgrace is real but obscure enough that
an article risks leaning on the king's general reputation, which is
reputation-reading, not a deed. The proposed replacement is Marcus
Aurelius raising Commodus to co-Augustus in 177, breaking the adoptive
succession that had produced every good emperor of the century — the
doctrine at full strength, a saint holding a fall, at the cost of a
deliberate double-seating against the Six Poles table.*

## complacent_nepotist_modern

The strongest honest reading available for Denethor II is the
abandonment of the post at the pyre: at the height of the siege he
withdraws from the defence, takes the guard of the Citadel off the walls
to serve his own funeral, and lays down an office he has not resigned.
The act is real, Tolkien's, and it does end a watch. What must be said
plainly is that it ends it by despair rather than by slackness, and this
vertex's fall is slackness — the post kept as a chair. Facing this seat
at 09h is the Paralyzed Purist, itself under audit; both modern seats on
the 21h–09h diameter are the weakest pair on the wheel.

> *"But if the watchman see the sword come, and blow not the trumpet,
> and the people be not warned... his blood will I require at the
> watchman's hand."* (Ezekiel 33:6)

*Flag (audit: REPLACE). The canonized deed — "the refusal to light the
beacons" — DOES NOT EXIST in Tolkien: in The Return of the King Denethor
lights the beacons himself and sends the Red Arrow to Rohan; the refusal
is the film's invention. His real deeds are pride and despair, and a
vigilant madman is not a complacent one. He is also double-booked, since
he holds Favoritism on the LEGACY wheel. The proposed replacement is
Robert Baratheon — told at the small council that the crown is millions
of dragons in debt, he orders the Hand's tourney anyway and leaves the
governing to whoever will do it; the canon already records him as this
seat's alternate.*

## wise_statesman_archetypal

Joseph is seated by the disposal of absolute power in Genesis 45 and 50:
holding Egypt's grain and Egypt's office over the brothers who sold him,
he feeds and settles them, and states the ground to their faces — *"ye

that the injury is real, the power is total, and the repayment would
