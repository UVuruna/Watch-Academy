# Edge Figure Plates — the Eight New Edge Seats, Three Sets, Both Polarities

Forty-eight deed badges for the cells the Thirteen-Axes round added:
8 seats × 3 registers (archetypal, historical, modern) × 2 polarities.
The eight SEAT lancets and their circle companions are already written
in [Edge Cell Plates](../prompts/archetype/edges_prompts.md) and are NOT
reopened here. **This sheet is the PEOPLE** — the exact parallel of
[Character Figure Plates](character_figures_prompts.md) and
[Vertex Figure Plates](vertices_figures_prompts.md), which cover the
Rose's other two wheels.

**Drop path (the tree law):**
`assets/archetypes/edges/<set>/colored/<Stem>.png`, where `<set>` is the
register — `archetypal`, `historical` or `modern`. Stems are PascalCase
tokens joined by underscores, diacritics ASCII-folded, apostrophes and
periods dropped.

---

## The law this sheet obeys — Charter rule 6, the Deed Doctrine

**A figure on a seat is not a verdict on a life. It is ONE DEED, lifted
out and held up as the symbol of that seat.** Everything else about the
person is deliberately forgotten. The roster and the one-line hooks are
sealed in [The Cube Canon](../../CUBE.md) §The Rosters (Session 24); the
full arguments are the Encyclopedia's eight new cell pages (Session 25);
this sheet turns each hook into one drawable moment.

Two consequences bind every prompt below:

- **A moment, never a portrait.** Nothing here is a likeness study. Each
  badge holds the single act the seat is argued from, which is also the
  cleanest way past the copyright wall.
- **THE COLOUR LAW HOLDS HARDEST ON THE FALLS.** No colour is good and
  no colour is evil; a fall is a station on a line, not a sentence on a
  person. Two seats carry this weight visibly — Van Gogh on Despair
  (an illness is a tragedy, never a verdict) and the house-elf on
  Servility (who ends free) — and their prompts are written with no
  gore, no cruelty enjoyed and no mockery.

## The register — one badge, one act

Every plate is a **ROUND 1:1 stained-glass badge medallion** in its
seat's own Rose field colour, the same night-window family as the seat
lancets. **Positive seats:** the glass is clear and lit. **Fallen
seats:** the identical colour gone hard, close and airless. Leadwork and
palette per seat are inherited from the seat sheet and repeated in every
prompt, so no generation has to infer them.

| Seat | Rose field | Border braid |
|---|---|---|
| Prudence / Indifference | ember red | blue + moon-gray violet |
| Ardor / Vendetta | midnight blue | orange + gold |
| Steadfastness / Machination | burnt orange | blue + gold |
| Reform / Zealotry | pale moonlit cyan | orange + moon-gray violet |
| Meekness / Despair | living leaf-green | blue + green |
| Aspiration / Megalomania | pale moonlit rose | orange + dark red |
| Self-Mastery / Disdain | ember red | blue + dark red |
| Diligence / Servility | midnight blue | orange + green |

**House rules in every prompt:** photorealistic render, isolated
background, the circular window shape IS the frame, ASPECT RATIO exactly
1:1, at most ONE contained glow source with every other light dim and
matte, **NO lettering anywhere** — no signs, no notices, no readable
marks of any kind.

## Copyright routing — Charter rule 7

- **`_v1` named** — public domain, historical, biblical, mythological.
  The name may stand inside the fence.
- **`_v2` descriptive** — protected material. **No name, no franchise
  term, ever, inside a fence.** The deed carries the plate.
- **`_v3` reference** — a likeness attached by PromptPainter's `←` line.
  **This sheet has ZERO `_v3` entries** — every modern seat's deed proved
  drawable without a likeness, so the owner supplies no reference stills.

A protected name inside a fenced prompt is a defect. Headings, notes and
tables outside the fences carry the real names — that is intended, and
it is how the owner reads the sheet.
