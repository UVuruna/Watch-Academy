# Character Figure Plates — the Legacy Wheel's Forty-Eight Seats, Three Sets

The LEGACY wheel of the Rose is the **Character** compass — eight
directions, each holding a virtue and its fall, each seated by THREE
people (archetypal, historical, modern) in BOTH polarities: 48 cells.
The eight direction lancets and their eight round companions are already
written in [Character Archetypes](../prompts/archetype/character_prompts.md)
and are NOT reopened here. This sheet is the PEOPLE.

**Drop path (the tree law):**
`assets/archetypes/character/<set>/colored/<Stem>.png`, where `<set>` is
the register — `archetypal`, `historical` or `modern`. Stems are
PascalCase tokens joined by underscores, diacritics ASCII-folded,
apostrophes and periods dropped.

---

## The law this sheet obeys — Charter rule 6, the Deed Doctrine

**A figure on a seat is not a verdict on a life. It is ONE DEED, lifted
out and held up as the symbol of that seat.** Everything else about the
person is deliberately forgotten. The owner's own example: Stalin sits on
steadfastness for the refusal to trade his captured son — *"I will not
trade a lieutenant for a general"* — and nothing else about Stalin exists
on that seat. The seat does not claim the man was steadfast; it claims
THAT ACT was.

Three consequences govern every prompt below:

1. **The image is the ACT, never a portrait.** No figure is drawn seated
   for a likeness; each is drawn mid-deed. A monster may hold a virtue
   and a saint may hold a fall — that is the doctrine working.
2. **The act is also the copyright answer.** We are not drawing a
   protected character; we are drawing a moment. That is what drops most
   of the modern set from a reference route to a plain descriptive one.
3. **A seat with no nameable, checkable deed is a WRONG SEAT.** Where the
   deed audit returned *weak* or *replace*, the entry is still written for
   the figure the canon currently seats, and an *italic note* states the
   audit's concern and its proposed replacement. **The owner decides; the
   sheet does not decide for him.**

## The register — one badge, one act

Every plate is a **ROUND 1:1 stained-glass badge medallion** in its
direction's own colour, the same night-window family as the wheel's own
circles. **Positive seats:** the glass is clear and lit. **Fallen seats:**
the identical colour gone hard, close and airless. Leadwork and palette
per arm are inherited from the wheel sheet and repeated in every prompt,
so no generation has to infer them.

**House rules in every prompt:** photorealistic render, isolated
background, the circular window shape IS the frame, ASPECT RATIO exactly
1:1, at most ONE contained glow source with every other light dim and
matte, **NO lettering anywhere**.

## Copyright routing — Charter rule 7

- **`_v1` named** — public domain, historical, biblical, mythological.
  The name may stand inside the fence.
- **`_v2` descriptive** — protected material. **No name, no franchise
  term, ever, inside a fence.** The deed carries the plate.
- **`_v3` reference** — a likeness attached by PromptPainter's `←` line.
  **This sheet has ZERO `_v3` entries** — every modern seat's deed proved
  drawable without any likeness, so the owner supplies no stills. See the
  [reference inventory](#reference-inventory) at the end.

A protected name inside a fenced prompt is a defect. Headings, notes and
tables outside the fences carry the real names — that is intended, and it
is how the owner reads the sheet.
