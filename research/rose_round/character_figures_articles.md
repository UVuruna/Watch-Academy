# Character Figure Articles — the Legacy Wheel's Forty-Eight Seats

One argument per figure on the LEGACY (Character) wheel: 8 directions ×
3 registers (archetypal, historical, modern) × 2 polarities = 48 entries.
Every article obeys Charter rule 6 — **the subject is the ACT, and the
person's name is the label on the deed.** Where the deed audit returned
*weak* or *replace*, the article is written honestly for the figure the
canon currently seats and closes with an italic line stating the
difficulty; the owner decides, the sheet does not.

**Key scheme:** `<seat>_<register>` — the seat name is the virtue for a
positive figure and the fall for a fallen one, so all 48 keys are unique
and none collides with the eight arm articles
(`articles.archetype_compass_character`) or with the Prophecy wheel's
vertex keys.

## loyalty_archetypal

The act that seats Penelope is the unpicking of Laertes' shroud: a promise to choose among the suitors when the burial cloth is finished, and then three years of undoing by night exactly what was woven by day, until a servant discloses the method (*Odyssey* II.93–110; XIX.137–156). What makes this Loyalty rather than mere endurance is that the bond is kept by LABOUR: every night's undoing is a fresh refusal, taken again a thousand times, against a household in which law, numbers and the ordinary expectation that a widow remarries all stand on the other side, and paid for in an estate eaten through while the refusal continues. Loyalty is the positive pole of the Judgment axis — judgment resting on a bond rather than on a rule — and yellow crowns it at noon as the covenant colour. Its fall on the same arm is the same nightly choice made for a category instead of a person. Directly across the wheel Abraham refuses a king's goods on a principle owed to nobody: the same steadfastness with the ground changed, which is precisely what the 12h–24h diameter argues about. *"Many waters cannot quench love, neither can the floods drown it"* (Song of Solomon 8:7).

## loyalty_historical

Ernest Shackleton is seated by the return for the men on Elephant Island. With *Endurance* crushed and the expedition's purpose already lost, he took the open boat *James Caird* eight hundred nautical miles across the Southern Ocean between 24 April and 10 May 1916, crossed South Georgia on foot, and then made three failed relief attempts before the Chilean tug *Yelcho* lifted all twenty-two men off on 30 August 1916. The deed is this seat because the objective was gone and the bond was not: four months and four ships were spent on nothing recoverable except a promise, by a commander who had no remaining expedition to justify the expense. Loyalty is a judgment that keeps its ground when the reason of state has evaporated, and here the arithmetic is checkable — twenty-two of twenty-two, to the day. The seat's fall would have been to save the survivors who were his own and count the rest as loss; the line is never approached. Facing him at 24h, Socrates refuses on principle what Shackleton pursues on covenant. *"If thou forbear to deliver them that are drawn unto death... doth not he that pondereth the heart consider it?"* (Proverbs 24:11–12).

## loyalty_modern

Samwise Gamgee is seated by one sentence acted rather than said: unable to take the burden on the slopes of Orodruin, he lifts the bearer instead — *"I can't carry it for you, but I can carry you"* — and climbs. The deed is Loyalty measured against its exact limit. He does not claim the Ring, does not pretend he could hold it, and does not substitute himself for the man he serves; he identifies the one thing still in his power and spends his body on it. That is the discriminator this seat needs, because loyalty that offers only what it wants to offer is preference, and loyalty that seizes the office of the one it serves is usurpation. Yellow's virtue is a bond honoured when it has stopped being useful to the one honouring it. Across the wheel Batman keeps a rule at its most expensive; here a man keeps a person at his own weight, and the two are the wheel's two answers to the same pressure. *"Bear ye one another's burdens, and so fulfil the law of Christ"* (Galatians 6:2).

## tribalism_archetypal

David holds the archetypal fall of the covenant by the recall of Absalom: the son who murdered Amnon is fetched home from exile in Geshur, restored to the king's presence and kissed (2 Samuel 14:21–33), after the same king had already declined to act on the rape of Tamar (13:21). The deed is an act, not an omission, which is what makes it seatable — and its force is comparative. The reign that executed a stranger on his own word for touching Saul (2 Samuel 1:15) suspends the law of blood twice inside one household. Tribalism is not the hatred of outsiders; it is the closure of the covenant, one law for us and another for everyone else, and the closure is here performed by the man who wrote *"the LORD render to every man his righteousness"*. This is Penelope's drive continued past its measure: the bond that should GROUND a judgment has replaced it, and the line is crossed at the exact point where kinship stops being the reason to weigh a case and becomes the reason not to. Its opposite at 24h is Javert, who can make no exception at all. *"Ye shall not respect persons in judgment; but ye shall hear the small as well as the great"* (Deuteronomy 1:17).

## tribalism_historical

The seat's historical fall is argued on the bull *Inter caetera* of 4 May 1493, in which Alexander VI drew a meridian across an ocean nobody had crossed and assigned every land and people beyond it to Castile, with the duty of subjecting *"barbarous nations"* attached. The deed has this seat's shape exactly: a single instrument in which the standing of a human being is determined by which side of a line he was born on, issued over one signature and enforceable as law. Tribalism's whole content is that the covenant has an outside, and a chancery that can rule the outside into existence with a pen has said so more plainly than any massacre. Yellow gone brassy is the colour of a covenant that has become a boundary. Across the wheel Robespierre applies one law to everybody including his friends; here one man applies two laws by geography, which is the same failure of judgment approached from the opposite side. *"The stranger that dwelleth with you shall be unto you as one born among you, and thou shalt love him as thyself"* (Leviticus 19:34).

*Flagged seat: the audit returns **WEAK**. `Inter caetera` was drafted at Spanish request and follows Nicholas V's* Romanus Pontifex *(1455) — a policy signed rather than an act committed — and the deed the roster actually leans on (Cesare made cardinal at eighteen; the Romagna turned into a family patrimony) is NEPOTISM, which is this wheel's own neighbouring seat at 15h. A fall that borrows the adjacent arm's definition is not seated. The audit's replacement is **Urban II** and the sermon at Clermont, 27 November 1095: remission of sins promised to men who went east to kill, with killing made meritorious or criminal by which side of a line the killed stood on — the covenant closed, with a date and a place.*

## tribalism_modern

Bellatrix Lestrange is seated by the branding at Malfoy Manor: the word for a blood category cut into a captive girl's forearm with a knife. The deed argues the fall better than any of the murders around it, because it is not violence done to a person but violence done to a CLASSIFICATION — the victim is first converted into a member of a set, and the set is then written on her body where it cannot be removed. That is tribalism's mechanism laid bare: the covenant closes by deciding in advance what a body is, so that no judgment about the person inside it is ever required. It is Samwise's own drive walked past measure — total, unquestioning attachment — with the object changed from a man to a category and a master, and the line is crossed the moment the bond stops attaching to anyone in particular. Facing her at 24h, Judge Dredd applies a code to a face identical to his own; here a code is applied to a face that has been erased first. *"Act so that you treat humanity, whether in your own person or in that of another, always as an end and never merely as a means"* (Kant, *Groundwork*).

## patronage_archetypal

Boaz is seated by the redemption bought at the gate of Bethlehem: ten elders seated as witnesses, the nearer kinsman offered Elimelech's field first, and when he withdraws because the field carries a foreign widow with it, Boaz buys both — publicly, under law, on the record (Ruth 4:1–10). Patronage is Loyalty joined to Dignity, a bond honoured from a position of strength, and this act is that definition executed: a man under no obligation converts his standing into a legal fact for two women who have none, and does it in the one setting where the obligation can afterwards be enforced against him. The discriminator against the fall on the same arm is that the transfer is made by rule and in the open, not by preference and in private. Orange is the true child of the yellow and red that flank it, and the seat is the Cube's Steady Guardian read flat. Across the wheel John the Baptist subtracts himself from a work he keeps serving; Boaz adds his weight to a claim that is not his — the same asymmetry of standing, spent in the two possible directions. *"I was a father to the poor: and the cause which I knew not I searched out"* (Job 29:16).

## patronage_historical

Lorenzo de' Medici is seated by one decision taken around 1489: the fourteen-year-old Michelangelo is removed from Ghirlandaio's workshop into the Medici palace, given a room, a stipend, a place at the family table and the run of the San Marco garden (Condivi; Vasari). The act is Patronage and not Favoritism because of where the weight is spent — on the WORK, and on a stonecutter's son with no claim of blood, marriage or faction on the household that lends him its rank. The cost is real and the ranking is deliberate: a boy of no family is seated among men whose position depends on family, by the one person who could make that stick. Patronage answers the wheel's permanent question about what a person with weight does with people who have none, and the answer here is checkable in marble rather than in gratitude. Its fall stands on the same arm and differs by a single substitution — the bond in place of the work. Facing him at 03h, Thomas More hands an office back; Lorenzo hands one out. *"Seest thou a man diligent in his business? he shall stand before kings"* (Proverbs 22:29).

## patronage_modern

Charles Xavier is seated by the opening of his own inherited house to a hunted child: an orphaned boy being run as an asset by a criminal handler is given a room in an ancestral estate, a name and a place, and the estate itself is converted from a private fortune into a shelter for children the state would have institutionalised. The deed's shape is this seat's: standing that could have been kept is spent on those who have none, and it is spent structurally rather than as a favour — what is transferred is not money but membership. Orange holds the arm because the blend is exact here, a bond kept (yellow) by someone who knows his own weight (red). Its fall on the same arm would be to raise a favourite instead of a class of the unprotected. Facing him at 03h, Obi-Wan Kenobi withdraws every trace of himself from a service he continues; Xavier does the opposite and puts his name over the door so that others may shelter under it. *"I was a stranger, and ye took me in"* (Matthew 25:35).

*Flagged seat: the audit returns **WEAK**. The seat here rests on a PREMISE rather than an act — "he founded a school" is a standing condition, not a deed done under pressure at a cost, and the founding is told differently in every continuity, so no single checkable act carries it. The Patronage shape is genuinely his; no one deed is. The audit's replacement is **Uncle Iroh**: a crown prince and general, first in line to a throne, boarding a banished boy's ship on the day of the banishment and following him into exile — his own rank spent at once and completely on a nephew who has none and does not want him there.*

## favoritism_archetypal

King Lear is seated by the love-test: a kingdom apportioned according to which daughter praises him best, and the one who refuses to bid disinherited on the spot, her third divided between the two who did (I.i). The act is Favoritism at full strength because the patron's weight is genuinely his to spend and he spends it on the bond rather than on the work — the criterion applied to the government of a country is affection performed aloud, and the only candidate who declines to perform is the only one removed. That is the exact point at which raising your own becomes preferring your own: not the loving, but the substitution of loving for judging. Orange gone airless is Patronage's colour hoarded. Its virtue on the same arm, Boaz, transfers standing by law in front of witnesses; Lear transfers a kingdom by feeling in front of courtiers. Across the wheel, Father Ferapont produces his own discipline as evidence; Lear demands that others produce theirs. *"He may not make the son of the beloved firstborn before the son of the hated, which is indeed the firstborn"* (Deuteronomy 21:16).

*Flagged seat: the audit returns **WEAK — collision, not quality**. The deed is perfect for the seat and fails a different law: this figure on this exact act is already sealed on the PROPHECY wheel, where the Complacent Nepotist article reads "Lear's seat is the map." One deed cannot hold two seats; under the deed doctrine that is not a shared figure but the same picture twice, and Charter rule 5 forbids it. No other Lear act carries this weight. The audit's replacement is **Jacob**: the coat of many colours made for Joseph because he loved him more than all his other sons (Genesis 37:3–4) — partiality manufactured as a visible garment and worn daily in front of the brothers it excludes, with the whole catastrophe of the house following from that one gift.*

## favoritism_historical

Nicolae Ceaușescu is seated by the elevation of Elena in 1980 to First Deputy Prime Minister, after her installation in the Political Executive Committee and her titling as *"academician doctor engineer"* on chemistry papers written by other people; their son Nicu was made first secretary of Sibiu county and head of the Communist Youth. The deed is this fall's definition performed with the receipts attached: the state's second office is transferred to a household member, and the fabricated credentials prove the transfer was known to be against desert at the moment it was made. Favoritism is the patron's weight spent on the bond instead of on the work, and the manufacture of a scientific reputation is the clearest possible admission that the work was understood to be missing. No verdict on the regime is required for the seat; one appointment and one honorary title carry it. Facing him at 03h, Simone Weil subtracts from herself what is owed to nobody; here everything is added to those who are owed nothing. *"There is an evil which I have seen under the sun... folly is set in great dignity"* (Ecclesiastes 10:5–6).

## favoritism_modern

Denethor II is seated by the weighing of his two sons in front of the living one — *"would that he had been in your place"* — and by what follows from it: Faramir is sent to retake Osgiliath on an errand the steward has himself called hopeless. The deed is Favoritism at its lethal end, because the allocation being made is not of rank or money but of a life, and the criterion is not capacity but preference for a dead man. The patron's own gift, the power to place people, is retained intact; only the standard has been replaced. That is where the line is crossed: Lorenzo's household spent its weight on what a boy could do, and here a house spends a son on what another son is remembered as having been. Orange hoarded is the colour of a stewardship kept for its holder's grief. Across the wheel Silas keeps his accounting in his own flesh; Denethor keeps his in someone else's. *"Fathers, provoke not your children to anger, lest they be discouraged"* (Colossians 3:21).

## dignity_archetypal

King Arthur is seated on the lament of Malory XX: told that the queen is taken and the fellowship broken, he grieves the knights above the queen — *"much more I am sorrier for my good knights' loss than for the loss of my fair queen"*. The seat's claim is that the king estimates correctly what he is: the office he holds is a fellowship rather than a marriage, and at the moment of maximum private injury he names the public loss as the greater one, without apology and without pretending the private one is small. Dignity on this wheel is the accurate valuation of one's own worth, and an office valued above its holder's grief is that accuracy applied inward. Red anchors the Self-Regard axis in the Scale's own colours. Its fall on the same arm confuses the office with the man; here they are told apart at the precise moment when confusing them would have been forgivable. Facing him at 06h, Jesus sets rank aside to wash feet: worth held down against worth held up, one axis, two directions. *"A good name is rather to be chosen than great riches"* (Proverbs 22:1).

*Flagged seat: the audit returns **WEAK**. The seat asks for worth held without apology under pressure, and Arthur's three canonical acts are the wrong shapes — the sword in the stone is election, something done TO him; the Round Table is an institution rather than an act; Camlann is ruin. What remains is a sentence rather than a deed at cost, and a seat that survives by assembling a legend is exactly what the deed doctrine forbids. The audit's replacement is **Queen Vashti**: ordered on the seventh day of the feast at Shushan to come and be displayed before the princes, she refuses and is deposed by decree for it (Esther 1:10–12) — one act, no explanation offered, total cost, in a single verse.*

## dignity_historical

Nelson Mandela is seated by the answer to P. W. Botha of February 1985. Offered conditional release if he renounced violence, he refused and had the refusal read aloud by his daughter Zindzi at Jabulani Stadium on 10 February — *"Only free men can negotiate; prisoners cannot enter into contracts"* — and served five more years. The deed is the cleanest statement of this seat in the whole roster because the price is known in advance, the door is standing open, and the refusal is issued not as a personal preference but as a statement about status: a man in custody cannot be a party to an agreement, so the offer is void by its own terms. Authority is worn here as an office rather than owned as a possession, which is the discriminator against the fall on the same arm. Red is the colour of worth claimed openly. Across the wheel Gandhi assigns himself the work of the lowest caste; the same axis, measured downward. *"To renounce liberty is to renounce being a man, to surrender the rights of humanity and even its duties"* (Rousseau, *The Social Contract* I.4).

## dignity_modern

T'Challa is seated by the repudiation of his own house's concealment: the crime of his throne is named to his father's face on the ancestral plane — *"You were wrong; all of you were wrong"* — and the concealment itself is ended publicly before an assembly of nations. The deed argues Dignity because the worth being asserted needs no pretence to survive. A house that hides its capacity is valuing itself by what others would think of it; a king who discloses the capacity and the crime together is claiming that the throne is worth more than its cover story. That is the correct estimate held without apology, and it is expensive — the disclosure is irreversible and the confession is voluntary. Red holds the arm; the fall beside it takes the same self-assertion and removes the object it was asserted for. Facing him at 06h, Superman places real power under someone else's authority; T'Challa refuses to hide real power at all. Both are accuracy, taken in the two directions the axis allows. *"He that covereth his sins shall not prosper: but whoso confesseth and forsaketh them shall have mercy"* (Proverbs 28:13).

*Flagged seat: the audit returns **WEAK**. The shape is right and no single act carries it — the Warrior Falls challenge is compliance with law rather than choice, the sparing of M'Baku is mercy, the stand-down from Zemo is composure, and the two scenes above must be read together before either becomes dignity. A seat that needs two scenes is a description of a man. The audit's replacement is **Maximus Decimus Meridius**: asked his name in the Colosseum by the emperor who had his family killed, he removes the helmet and states it in full — the slave's anonymity discarded and the worth declared without apology, at the maximum available price, in one gesture.*

## self_worship_archetypal

Lucifer holds this fall on the only deed on the dial recorded in the first person: the five resolutions of Isaiah 14:13–14 — *"I will ascend into heaven, I will exalt my throne above the stars of God... I will be like the most High."* Here the act and the sentence are the same object, which is why nothing further about the figure is needed or available. Self-Worship is Dignity continued until the office and its holder are confused, and the confusion is not inferred from behaviour but announced as a programme: the throne is to be moved, and the standard against which the move is measured is the mover. The line is crossed at the fifth clause, where likeness to the highest is stated as an intention rather than received as a gift — worth still correctly estimated, but now with nothing above it to be estimated against. Red gone airless anchors the axis. Its true diagonal is Humility at 06h, and the pair Judas–Lucifer is the strongest on the dial: the self set at zero against the self set at everything. *"Two cities have been formed by two loves: the earthly by the love of self, even to the contempt of God"* (Augustine, *City of God* XIV.28).

## self_worship_historical

Salvador Dalí is seated by his answer to the Surrealist tribunal of 5 February 1934. Summoned to Breton's flat to account for his work and his flirtation with Hitler, he appeared claiming fever, defended himself by declaring that he alone was the movement — *"La différence entre les surréalistes et moi, c'est que moi je suis surréaliste"* — and accepted expulsion. The deed is this seat rather than mere vanity because a choice was actually put and actually answered: a movement offered a man the option of subordinating himself to it, and he replied that the movement was a property of his person. The office and the holder are merged in one sentence, and the price — expulsion from the group that had made him — is paid on the spot. Red gone hard is the colour of a worth that has stopped needing an external measure. Its virtue on the same arm holds authority as an office; here there is no office left, only an identity. Facing him at 06h, Kafka orders his own work destroyed unread. *"For men to search their own glory is not glory"* (Proverbs 25:27).

## self_worship_modern

Lord Voldemort is seated by the authorship of his own name: at sixteen he murders his father and grandparents at Little Hangleton to erase the name he was given, then rearranges the letters of that name into a title of his own and requires it of everyone. The deed is Self-Worship at its strict definition, which is not overrating oneself but AUTHORING oneself — the origin is killed so that nothing precedes the self, and the new designation is issued rather than earned or received. Every later atrocity is a footnote to that one operation. The line is crossed at the murder, not the anagram: a man may rename himself, but only one who has decided that he is his own source needs the previous name to stop existing. Red hoarded is the arm's colour, and its virtue is worth correctly estimated against something outside it. Across the wheel Darth Vader concludes that his own worth is beyond recovery; here a man concludes there was nothing before him to recover. *"Let us make us a name, lest we be scattered abroad upon the face of the whole earth"* (Genesis 11:4).

## conviction_archetypal

Elijah is seated by the confrontation in Naboth's vineyard: the prophet walks in on Ahab in possession of the murdered smallholder's land and accuses him to his face — *"Hast thou killed, and also taken possession?"* (1 Kings 21:17–20). Conviction is Integrity joined to Dignity, and its discriminator against ordinary defiance is the direction of the refusal: it is addressed to one's OWN side. This is not a foreign tyrant but Israel's king; the case has no constituency, the victim is a single landowner already dead, and the accusation is delivered alone and in person. Carmel is the more famous act and is aimed outward, at other men's gods; the vineyard is the one that fits this seat, because it costs a prophet his standing with the only court he has. Pink is red thinned by the moonlight of the pole beside it, and the seat is the Cube's Wise Statesman read flat. Across the wheel Moses wishes his own office universal; Elijah exercises his against the man who granted it. *"But truly I am full of power by the spirit of the LORD... to declare unto Jacob his transgression, and to Israel his sin"* (Micah 3:8).

## conviction_historical

Martin Luther is seated at Worms on 18 April 1521: given a day to reconsider and asked once more to recant, he refused before Charles V — *"Unless I am convinced by the testimony of Scripture or by plain reason... Here I stand, I can do no other"* — and was outlawed by the Edict. The sequence is what makes this Conviction rather than stubbornness: the principle, that assent is owed only to Scripture and plain reason, existed before the stand and dictated it, and it is stated in the refusal itself as the condition on which he WOULD recant. The refusal is again addressed to his own Church and his own emperor, at the price of outlawry, and it is dated to the day. Pink holds the arm because this is dignity carried by a rule rather than by a person. The fall beside it keeps a principle after it has stopped being examined; here the examination is the principle. Facing him at 09h, Janusz Korczak refuses an exemption for the sake of a bond; Luther refuses one for the sake of a ground. *"We ought to obey God rather than men"* (Acts 5:29).

## conviction_modern

Steve Rogers is seated by the broadcast from inside: he takes the agency's own public-address system, tells every employee of the organisation he serves that it has been hollowed out from within, orders them to refuse the launch, and then grounds its fleet. The deed satisfies this seat's two tests at once. The refusal is aimed at his own side rather than at an enemy, and it is delivered in the institution's own voice — the loyalty is not withdrawn, it is exercised against the institution's present holders on behalf of what the institution was. And the principle precedes the act: the rule is stated aloud first and the fleet is grounded second, which is the wheel's own criterion for conviction over stubbornness. Pink is dignity thinned to the width of a principle. Its fall on the same arm administers a doctrine that has stopped being examined; here the doctrine is restated in public precisely so that it can be. Across the wheel Alfred Pennyworth accepts a moral wound in silence for one man; Rogers accepts exile in public for a rule. *"But if the watchman see the sword come, and blow not the trumpet... his blood will I require at the watchman's hand"* (Ezekiel 33:6).

## dogmatism_archetypal

Creon is seated by the enforcement of his own edict after every reason for it has been withdrawn: Polyneices proclaimed unburied on pain of death, and when his own niece performs the rites, she is walled up alive — Haemon refused, the elders refused, and the reversal made only after Teiresias, when it is too late for anyone. The deed is Dogmatism rather than firmness because of its timing. The decree had a reason on the day it was issued; what seats him is the enforcement carried out when the reason has been publicly dismantled and holding the line has become the identity instead of the policy. That is where the line is crossed: not at the decree but at the sentence executed against argument. Pink gone hard is Conviction's colour with the examination removed. Its virtue on the same arm accuses a king on a principle; here a king turns a principle into a person's grave. Facing him at 09h, Samson hands himself to a bond that has already betrayed him; Creon hands a girl to a rule that has already died. *"Summum ius summa iniuria" — "the highest law is the highest injustice"* (Cicero, *De Officiis* I.33).

## dogmatism_historical

Joseph Stalin is seated by the dismissal of Merkulov's report of 17 June 1941, returned in his own hand with an order that no measures be taken — the treatment already given to Sorge's warnings and to dozens of others, until 22 June. The deed is the operational form of this fall: a fact is refused because the doctrine forbids it, in writing, with the evidence physically on the desk. Dogmatism is Conviction past examination, and no clearer instance exists than a documentary refusal to revise an assessment that events are five days from disproving. An equal alternate exists in his personal editing of Lysenko's report to the VASKhNIL session of August 1948, after which an entire science was banned by decree. Pink hardened is a principle worn as identity. The seat also demonstrates the doctrine at work: the same man's refusal to trade a captured lieutenant for a field marshal is a virtue elsewhere, and neither statement is a verdict on the life. Across the wheel Marcus Antonius spends an empire on an attachment; here a man spends an army on a belief. *"The human understanding when it has once adopted an opinion draws all things else to support and agree with it"* (Bacon, *Novum Organum* I.46).

## dogmatism_modern

Dolores Umbridge is seated by the detention with the blood quill: a boy is set the line *"I must not tell lies"* and the words are cut into the back of his own hand by his own writing. The deed is Dogmatism made physical. The doctrine is never argued — it is administered, strictly, by the regulation, with a punishment whose entire mechanism converts the child into the parchment on which the official version is recorded. Nothing is asked and nothing is examined; the ruling simply becomes the boy's body. That is the crossing point of this arm: Steve Rogers states his principle so that it can be tested, and this seat states its principle so that it cannot be. Pink gone hard and airless is the colour. Its virtue on the same arm holds a rule with enough self-worth to stand on it alone; here a rule is held with enough authority never to have to. Facing her at 09h, Severus Snape administers a whole life as penance for one attachment; both administer, and neither examines. *"For they bind heavy burdens and grievous to be borne, and lay them on men's shoulders; but they themselves will not move them with one of their fingers"* (Matthew 23:4).

## integrity_archetypal

Abraham is seated by the refusal at the valley of Shaveh: after the rescue of Lot the king of Sodom offers him the recovered goods, and he refuses every item — *"I will not take from a thread even to a shoelatchet... lest thou shouldest say, I have made Abram rich"* (Genesis 14:22–23). The deed is Integrity because the principle is applied where no bond exists and against his own interest: the claim is legitimate, already earned in battle, and it is refused entire so that no one can afterwards price him. That is this pole's whole content — a cost accepted rather than an exception made — and it is decided before there is any pressure to decide it. The companion act has the same shape: *"Shall not the Judge of all the earth do right?"* (Genesis 18:25), a rule pressed on God himself. The binding of Isaac, the more famous act, is obedience rather than integrity, and the roster is right not to lean on it. Moon-gray violet is the colour of a law that does not care who is asking. Across the wheel Penelope keeps a bond by labour; here a man keeps a rule by loss. *"If I have made gold my hope, or have said to the fine gold, Thou art my confidence..."* (Job 31:24).

## integrity_historical

Socrates is seated on two refusals that hold the same rule against opposite regimes. As presiding epistates of the prytany in 406 BC he alone declined to put the illegal collective trial of the Arginusae generals to the vote, against an assembly in fury (*Apology* 32b; Xenophon, *Hellenica* I.7.15); in 404, ordered by the Thirty to fetch Leon of Salamis for execution, he simply went home (*Apology* 32c–d). Each is a single dated act with lethal exposure attached, and together they supply the proof this seat requires: the ground of the refusal is the procedure itself, not the side, because the democracy and the oligarchy received exactly the same answer. Integrity is judgment resting on principle rather than on bonds, and a principle that survives a change of masters has demonstrated that it is one. Moon-gray violet is midnight's own colour. Its fall on the same arm keeps the rule until the rule consumes its purpose; here the rule is kept only at the cost of the man holding it. Facing him at 12h, Shackleton spends four ships on a promise; both refuse to be released by circumstance. *"He who will fight for the right... must have a private station and not a public one"* (Plato, *Apology* 32a).

## integrity_modern

## renunciation_archetypal

## renunciation_historical

Thomas More is seated by the return of the Great Seal on 16 May 1532, the day after the Submission of the Clergy: the highest office in England resigned in a day, before any charge was laid, with no compulsion applied and no audience assembled, and a household left that he could no longer support. ## renunciation_modern

## mortification_archetypal

## mortification_historical
