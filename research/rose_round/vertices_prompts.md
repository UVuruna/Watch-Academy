## The colour law — read this before generating anything

Two colours are in play in every window and they must not be confused.

1. **The ARM HUE is the Rose's own palette** (`defaults.ROSE_PALETTE`,
   clockwise from 12h: yellow, orange, red, rose, purple-gray, cyan,
   blue, green). It is the dominant glass of the whole window. **Rose is
   RED THINNED BY MOONLIGHT** — pale, cold, silvered, never candy pink;
   **cyan is BLUE THINNED BY MOONLIGHT** — pale cold blue-green, dusty,
   never neon or swimming-pool; **purple-gray is the moon-gray violet of
   the Y-negative pole (~`#666699`)**, never royal or magenta purple.
2. **The THREE POLE COLOURS are the CUBE's own dress** — blue/orange for
   Activation, yellow/purple-gray for Judgment, green/red for
   Self-Regard. They appear ONLY in the window's two combination
   devices, never as the field colour. (This is where this wheel parts
   from the Character wheel, which wears the Scale's blue-and-red
   horizontal instead. On the Prophecy wheel the Cube dresses itself.)

**The combination devices (identical construction on all eight
lancets, so the reader learns them once):**

- **The apex trefoil** — three small panes set in the lancet's pointed
  head, one per axis, in that vertex's three pole colours. Coloured
  glass, dim and matte; never a light source.
- **The three-strand braid** — the border leadwork is a braided cord of
  the same three colours running the full height of the window.
- **THE FALL IS ONE COLOUR SWALLOWING THE OTHER TWO.** In the lower
  storey exactly ONE of the three has bled down over the whole glass and
  drowned its two partners; the braid below runs single-stranded in that
  colour with the other two strands cut. Which colour floods is derived
  from the vertex's own written article and is stated in each prompt.

## Rule #19 derivation check (written, root `CLAUDE.md` Rule #19)

**IRREDUCIBLE — generated (16 files):** eight VERTEX SEATS that exist
nowhere else in this project as images. Each is a new composition
holding three named forces meeting in one frame and, below the
leadwork, the one force swallowing the other two — a scene, not a
variant of any other scene. Their eight round companions condense the
upper reading for the small badge slot, which no crop of a 1:2 lancet
can do (a square crop of a two-storey window either loses the fall or
loses the subject). The vertex/fall pair is deliberately ONE window,
not two files: the fall is the same corner walked past its measure.

**COMPUTED — never generated:**

- **THE ROSE'S GEOMETRY** — three identical octa stars 15° apart, 8
  diamond arms at half-angle 22.5°, tip at the star radius, inner
  vertices at `tip / 2cos(half)`, neighbours overlapping by design.
  One star drawn three times at three rotations. Not one pixel of the
  geometry is art.
- **THE STAR OFFSETS AND Z-ORDER** — Prophecy's `−15° · +15° · 0°`
  (past deepest, future over it, present covering both) is three
  transform values in config, not three artworks.
- **THE EIGHT ARM COLOURS**, including rose and cyan as red and blue
  thinned toward moon-purple, are `ROSE_PALETTE` lookups. The wheel's
  coloured wedges are drawn by the renderer, never painted plates.
- **ROTATION, lit/ghost opacity, metal tints, the Diamond/Cube toggle**
  — transform and palette maths on these same files.
- **THE THREE FIGURE SETS ARE NOT COMPUTED** (Charter rule 5, owner
  decree 2026-07-27): three sets on one seat are three DIFFERENT PEOPLE
  holding one office, so each is its own queued image — in its own
  sheet, not this one. This sheet queues the SEATS; the people are a
  separate round.
- **THE FALL PLATE IS NOT A SEPARATE FILE** — it is the lower storey of
  the same window, and drawing it separately would enumerate what one
  composition already says.
