## The Three Axes (SEALED)

One 3D coordinate space — **the Cube** — describes CHARACTER, not
just morality: how much a person acts, on what they base judgment,
and how they hold themselves. Its six poles are **the dial's own six
arm colors**; the three axes are the hexagram's three diagonals.
Every axis names TWO readings per pole: the healthy expression and
the fallen extreme — a direction is a virtue in measure and a fall
past it (no "blind X" constructions: every fallen term stands alone,
strong and provocative — owner decree).

| Axis | Colors (pole ↔ pole) | Question | Healthy ↔ Healthy | Fallen ↔ Fallen |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **X — Activation** | Blue ↔ Orange | How little or how much do I act? | Composure ↔ Vigor | Lethargy ↔ Frenzy |
| **Y — Basis of Judgment** | Yellow ↔ Purple-Gray | Do bonds or principles rule my judgment? | Loyalty ↔ Integrity | Tribalism ↔ Legalism |
| **Z — Self-Regard** | Green ↔ Red | How do I hold my own worth? | Humility ↔ Dignity | Self-Annihilation ↔ Self-Worship |

Orientation (the owner's cube views): **Y is vertical** — Yellow the
top face, Purple-Gray the bottom; the Court corner shows **Yellow
top, Blue left (X), Red right (Z)**; the Genesis corner shows
**Purple-Gray bottom, Green (Z) and Orange (X)**. On the dial the
axes ARE the hexagram diagonals: 12h↔24h (Y), 16h↔04h (X),
20h↔08h (Z).

**The Purple-Gray hue law (SEALED).** The Y-negative pole is NEVER
royal purple: it is the muted, moon-gray violet the owner uses on
the Rose (the "Mesečev indigo" of the old compass, ~`#666699`) —
the Moon, midnight, the Winter Solstice. Wherever this canon says
Purple-Gray, that hue is meant.

**The seasonal two arcs of three (SEALED — owner correction: arcs of
THREE, non-overlapping, not fours).**

- **Waning arc** — Yellow → Orange → Red: Summer Solstice → Summer →
  Autumn; the year from its peak toward extinguishing, daylight
  shrinking.
- **Waxing arc** — Purple-Gray → Blue → Green: Winter Solstice →
  Winter → Spring; the year from its darkest seat toward the peak,
  daylight growing.

Each arc is LED by its solstice color; the two arcs together are the
dial's own year (the top of the wheel is already the summer
solstice).

**Character coordinates (SEALED as doctrine, implementation
queued).** Because the axes span a space, any figure can be
DESCRIBED as a point `(x, y, z)` — the chess-rules way (root Rule
19): a small set of axes covers every personality instead of
enumerating them. The Encyclopedia will grow per-figure coordinate
badges (a small cube glyph); the Cube is also the standing
instrument for describing ANY future entity (professions, themes,
figures).

midnight.

## The Article Charter (SEALED — the writing law)

Legends and Encyclopedia articles are written as **scientific-
philosophical papers, not belles-lettres**. We deal in a heritage of
abstractions centuries old; that is a reason for rigor, not for
fairy-tale prose (the owner: psychology is not less serious for
lacking the experiment).

1. **Structure of every article:** THESIS (the claim, first
   sentence) → ARGUMENT (why this figure/concept binds to THIS seat:
   the bundle match, the source, the historical fact) →
   CORRESPONDENCES (the web links: virtue, vice, emotion, weekday) →
   QUOTE (Bible or the great philosophers — the existing "Quote the
   greats" law).
2. **Tone:** claims are argued, never narrated; no charming little
   stories; consistent English terminology.
3. **Verse, metaphor, prayer:** allowed ONLY set apart — *italic,
   under quotation marks*, as an epigraph or closing citation —
   never woven into the argumentative text.
4. **NO SCENE DESCRIPTION (owner 2026-07-26):** an article NEVER
   describes what its image shows — no narrating the frame, the
   pose, the window, the composition. The picture stands beside the
   text; describing it is waste and belles-lettres in one. The
   article argues ONLY the symbolism: why this seat, why this color,
   why this virtue and vice, how it binds into the web. (The REWORK
   pass over the EXISTING articles that violated this ran in Session
   21, 2026-07-27: **21 articles rewritten** — the Court's Eye and the
   Prism's Seal, the Family's Heart, all four Temperaments, all four
   evangelist rows of the Tetramorph, Lucifer and Judas on the Prism,
   seven of the eight Walks and the Ages' Birth. What went out: art-
   asset talk ("his Scale window", "no new glass is cut", "his rondel
   keeps"), composition narration ("its iris", "the Heart in open
   hands", "the overlapping heart") and staged little scenes ("with
   the plough in hand", "the student at the lamp", "owning nothing but
   the road"). `tests/test_cube_encyclopedia.py` pins the exact
   phrases so they cannot return.)
