# Week Registry — Gaming

**Script:** [Gaming (script)](../gaming.py)

## Purpose

Two franchises, six casts — the same nine seats held three times over per franchise.

One entry per theme, in the shape [the package](__init__.md) defines.
The split follows the project's own hierarchy (`taxonomy.WEEK_GROUPS`),
so a theme's entry sits in the file its art sits under.

## Themes (6)

- `wow_alliance`
- `wow_horde`
- `wow_evil`
- `cp_gangs`
- `cp_street`
- `cp_corpo`

## Worth knowing

The Cyberpunk casts carry the registry's ROSTER seats: a seat that holds several named figures and turns through them. The Power cast's Throne, Mirror and Ninth turn TOGETHER on the ISO week's parity (`term_weekly`).
