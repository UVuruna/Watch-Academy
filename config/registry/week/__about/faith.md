# Week Registry — Faith

**Script:** [Faith (script)](../faith.py)

## Purpose

The creeds, seated by their OWN canon rather than by their rest day, and the three Scripture sets.

One entry per theme, in the shape [the package](__init__.md) defines.
The split follows the project's own hierarchy (`taxonomy.WEEK_GROUPS`),
so a theme's entry sits in the file its art sits under.

## Themes (5)

- `religion`
- `religion_alt`
- `bible`
- `bible2`
- `bible_dark`

## Worth knowing

Three themes share the `bible` folder and two share `creeds` — different registers of one folder, and three (or two) genuinely different casts, because the figures and the text differ.
