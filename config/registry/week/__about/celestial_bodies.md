# Week Registry — Celestial bodies

**Script:** [Celestial bodies (script)](../celestial_bodies.py)

## Purpose

The planets in their three depictions — the original photographs, the owner's glyphs, the bronze medallion — plus the deep sky and the world itself.

One entry per theme, in the shape [the package](__init__.md) defines.
The split follows the project's own hierarchy (`taxonomy.WEEK_GROUPS`),
so a theme's entry sits in the file its art sits under.

## Themes (5)

- `planets`
- `planet_signs`
- `cosmos`
- `planets_art`
- `continents`

## Worth knowing

The Continents are the one family whose seat stems carry `COMPUTED`: they ARE the dial's own Earth faces, resolved from the region table at derivation time.
