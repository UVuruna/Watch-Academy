# Week Registry — Cultures & crafts

**Script:** [Cultures & crafts (script)](../crafts.py)

## Purpose

The seven metals of alchemy, the Japanese yobi week, and the offices people hold — the trades and the boardroom.

One entry per theme, in the shape [the package](__init__.md) defines.
The split follows the project's own hierarchy (`taxonomy.WEEK_GROUPS`),
so a theme's entry sits in the file its art sits under.

## Themes (4)

- `alchemy`
- `japan`
- `profession`
- `corporate`

## Worth knowing

The Corporation is the one cast in the book whose members are OFFICES rather than persons.
