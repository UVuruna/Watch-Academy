# Week Registry — Animal societies

**Script:** [Animal societies (script)](../societies.py)

## Purpose

Three orders of order: the pack ranks, the hive works by age, the herd remembers.

One entry per theme, in the shape [the package](__init__.md) defines.
The split follows the project's own hierarchy (`taxonomy.WEEK_GROUPS`),
so a theme's entry sits in the file its art sits under.

## Themes (3)

- `wolf`
- `bee`
- `elephant`

## Worth knowing

Each seats its Sunday as a rank pair — Alpha and Omega, Queen and Cleaner, Matriarch and Memory.
