# Week Registry — Films

**Script:** [Films (script)](../films.py)

## Purpose

The Star Wars trio: the side that loses and is right, the side that wins, the generation that inherits the result.

One entry per theme, in the shape [the package](__init__.md) defines.
The split follows the project's own hierarchy (`taxonomy.WEEK_GROUPS`),
so a theme's entry sits in the file its art sits under.

## Themes (3)

- `sw_jedi`
- `sw_sith`
- `sw_dyad`

## Worth knowing

Three people hold seats in two casts at two ages each — six seats, six independent arguments. The Dyad's Ninth is the registry's first daylight/night switch.
