# Week Registry — Mythologies

**Script:** [Mythologies (script)](../myth.py)

## Purpose

The four pantheons whose gods name the weekdays, plus the Olympians' bestiary and the Chinese celestial court.

One entry per theme, in the shape [the package](__init__.md) defines.
The split follows the project's own hierarchy (`taxonomy.WEEK_GROUPS`),
so a theme's entry sits in the file its art sits under.

## Themes (6)

- `greek`
- `norse`
- `egypt`
- `slavic`
- `age_of_heroes`
- `celestial_court`

## Worth knowing

All four pantheons carry a SECOND roster (`pantheon`) — the culture's own hierarchy seated on our archetypes, with its own article set and its own dual.
