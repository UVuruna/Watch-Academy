# Week Registry — The Inner Wheel

**Script:** [The Inner Wheel (script)](../inner_wheel.py)

## Purpose

The days ARE their virtues, their sins, their hour-moods.

One entry per theme, in the shape [the package](__init__.md) defines.
The split follows the project's own hierarchy (`taxonomy.WEEK_GROUPS`),
so a theme's entry sits in the file its art sits under.

## Themes (3)

- `virtues`
- `sins`
- `moods`

## Worth knowing

Their art lives OUTSIDE `assets/weeks/<group>/`: the `../emblem/...` step-up reaches the emblem families, which is why their `art` paths begin with `..`.
