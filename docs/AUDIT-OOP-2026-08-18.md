## 7. Grades

**ONE KIND, ONE CLASS — 7/10.**

1. Where a kind is user-visible, a registry or a class already exists and adding
   an instance IS adding an object: 35 themes in one table
   (`config/registry/week.py:83`), 13 layers under one ABC
   (`render/context.py:99`), 9 watch-face sections in one tuple
   (`app/watch_face/window.py:45`), one card family used 14 times across 6
   modules.
2. But the largest table in the application is written by hand 56 times:
   `wrap(lambda v: self._set_display_choice("<key>", v))`,
   `app/controller.py:3294-3556`.
3. And two whole UI kinds have no base at all — 3 encyclopedia screens
   (`home.py:28`, `themes.py:26`, `reader.py:51`, with the zoom protocol forked
   into two spellings) and 8 dialogs repeating the same 4-line preamble.

**STRUCTURE — 5/10.**

1. Four source files are over the wall and all four are ratcheted; the two
   biggest were MAPPED for a split on 2026-08-01 and are still whole — and
   `app/controller.py` has since grown from the 3,449 lines its own ratchet
   entry quotes to 4,483.
2. Seven more files sit at 874-986 logic lines with no ratchet entry: the
   project is walking into the wall again, exactly what the law's "split before
   the wall reaches you" was written against.
3. Against that: `render/layers.py` (3,881 lines) really was split into 13 layer
   modules plus 12 responsibility modules and LEFT the ratchet; the one-way flow
   holds with a purity test; folder docs exist throughout; the config layer is
   genuinely tabular. The machinery works — the two big files are simply not
   being fed to it.
