# research/seating/

Generated output of [Seating Preview](../__about/seating_preview.md) —
offscreen previews of the two Session-26 seatings, for the owner's eyes
before any pointer is wired to either one. No `.md` of its own; images only,
regenerated by re-running the script.

## Files

- 🖼️ `rose_24.png` — the Rose of the Twenty-Four: three octa stars 15°
  apart, all 24 human seats plus the three sacred seats at the axle
- 🖼️ `calendar_12.png` — the Calendar of the Twelve: one human axis per
  month, each axis's two ends laid out by radius
