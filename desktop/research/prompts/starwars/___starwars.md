# prompts/starwars/

The Star Wars weekday theme (GAMING + CORPORATION SHEET WAVE, owner-sealed
rosters 2026-07-22) — three casts (Svetla/light, Tamna/dark, Nova/the dyad)
— plus two copyright-workaround variant sheets for the same 30 figures,
written after the parent sheet kept being refused by image generators for
naming protected characters and franchise terms.
